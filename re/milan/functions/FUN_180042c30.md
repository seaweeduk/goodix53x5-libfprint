## Recovered Behavior

The function validates enrollment state and feature status, then takes one of two paths. The first stage calls `FUN_180042610`; later stages deep-copy the incoming feature with `FUN_1800422e0`, assign its lifecycle ordinal, derive direct pair relations through `FUN_180041ac0`, and route those relations through `FUN_180042840`. It initializes the enrollment progress output, maintains the feature-index array, and calls finalization helpers only when the live count reaches the template's physical configured maximum.

Evidence addresses include the first-stage branch at `0x180042d8b`, copy/setup around `0x180042dc0`, direct relation construction at `0x180042e23`, graph processing at `0x180042e82`, and completion calls before `0x180042ec8`.

After each accepted enrollment stage it writes identity indices to order slots
`[0, feature_count)` at template `+0x87e8` and `UINT32_MAX` to the remaining
configured-capacity slots. Study append later changes this gallery order under a
different finalization contract.

Stages with no admitted direct relation are still accepted. When
`FUN_180041ac0` leaves the candidate count below one, the function sets progress
to `0x64000064`, retains the copied feature, and continues through order/count
handling. It does not roll back the feature or require graph establishment.
Evidence is `0x180042e23..0x180042e4e`.

For profile 9/type 12 that physical maximum is 40, established by
`FUN_180037c80`; it is not the mode-0 session's requested enrollment count. The
binary defaults at `0x1800f141c..0x1800f141e` are `08 0c 0c`: standard
`+0x41c == 8`, coating `+0x41d == 12`, and Glass `+0x41e == 12`. The default
adapter attach selects the Glass byte at
`FUN_18001e5b0:0x18001eaea..0x18001eafb`, carries it in context `+0x8a`, and
ultimately writes it to session required count `+0x08` at
`FUN_18002d280:0x18002d3b7..0x18002d3c7`;
`EngineAdapterSetEnrollmentParameters` does not override it. The other
enrollment-path eights are not default-profile overrides.

The adapter increments session used count separately and `FUN_18002d8b0` packs
when used equals requested. Normal enrollment therefore completes after 12
accepted stages without executing either capacity finalizer. Rejected or
rolled-back scans can require more physical touches but do not reduce that
accepted-stage requirement. Requested-8 oracle evidence exercises an explicit
legacy/nondefault count only; graph establishment on stage 8 is merely a
same-insertion observation because `FUN_180042180` publishes `f2/f5` immediately.

Current libfprint does not call this live update API. It independently completes
after `GOODIX_ENROLL_SAMPLES=12` and rebuilds the final packed template at
`drivers/goodix53x5/device/enroll.c:433-449`; that count matches the default DLL
adapter requirement, remains below 40, and has no native capacity-finalizer
analogue.

## Stage Mutation Order

- Before any mutation, `0x180042d28..0x180042d4c` rejects live count greater
  than or equal to physical maximum with `0x80000005`, or an incoming feature
  with zero primary records at `+0xf0` with `0x80000006`; progress is cleared.
- For every later stage, `0x180042d95..0x180042dc6` copies into slot
  `old_feature_count`, writes old relation count to feature `b6/+0x114`, clears
  `ba/+0x124` and `bd/+0x130`, writes stage identity to `bc/+0x12c`, and then
  increments feature count before relation construction. The qword clear at
  `+0x130` also clears `be/+0x134`.
- `FUN_180041ac0` then appends exactly one physical relation slot per prior
  feature, defined or unset. Thus after `n` accepted features live `+0x24` and
  packed tag `92` are exactly `1 + n*(n-1)/2`; row bases are
  `0,1,2,4,7,...`. These are matrix cardinality, not packed relation count.
- Graph processing runs after all direct slots and anti-fake pair updates for
  the stage. First-anchor success immediately writes `f2/+0x87d8`, both active
  `b5` markers, and `f5/+0x87e4`; `f3/f4` remain constructor values `-1/-1`.
  A later no-relation stage leaves established graph metadata and prior active
  markers unchanged while retaining the new inactive feature.
- Identity order is rewritten only after graph processing: `[0,n)` becomes
  identity and `[n,maximum)` becomes `UINT32_MAX`. At exact physical capacity,
  the call order is then `FUN_18007a040(context,NULL)` followed by
  `FUN_180047550(context)`; both returns are ignored.

For every admitted direct relation, `FUN_180041ac0` completes its anti-fake pair
update before graph acceptance. The update persists even when the edge remains
disconnected or a later adapter policy rolls the stage back; its exact record
gate and averaging arithmetic are owned by `FUN_180041ac0.md`.

Packing is a distinct projection. For type 12, `FUN_18003eaf0` emits no
relations when `f5` is zero. When established, it visits feature indices in
ascending order, skips `f2`, and emits each nonnegative feature-to-reference
slot; output count is therefore at most `n-1`, independent of triangular live
`+0x24`. Current `goodix_milan_relation_matrix_project_reference_star()` has the
same count, omission, and feature-order contract. Graphless output carries no
relations and publishes raw `f2/f3/f4/f5`; canonical enrollment publishes
`-1/-1/-1/0`.

## Ownership And Reconstruction Boundary

`FUN_180042c30` has no failure exit after it increments feature count. A
zero-relation or graphless stage is a successful mutation. Later mode-0 and
mode-1 policy rollback belongs to `enrolDeleteImage`/`FUN_180037a30`: it rewinds
the last row and counts, but does not restore anti-fake pair scores already
averaged into prior features. If the removed feature was `f2`, deletion clears
`f5` and all remaining active markers but leaves raw `f2/f3/f4` unchanged.

Current `goodix_match_combine_templates()` instead rebuilds a fresh context from
immutable packed singleton probes on every call
(`drivers/goodix53x5/milan/enrollment/template.c:157-422`). It commits no
partial mutation when it returns `NULL`.

The intended combiner inputs are current extractor singletons, not arbitrary
saved one-feature galleries. Natural extraction initializes enrollment-owned
`b5/b6/ba/bb/bc/bd/be` to zero; DLL copy and first-stage field ownership are
documented in `FUN_1800422e0.md` and `FUN_180042610.md`. Later stages replace
`b6`, clear `ba/bd/be`, write `bc` to the physical stage index, and let graph
processing own `b5`. Current reconstruction rewrites only `b5`, `b6`, and `bc`,
so exact natural behavior relies on the extractor-zero contract for the other
enrollment-owned fields. A saved singleton with `b7 == record_count` also
crosses the lossy DLL decoder boundary documented in `FUN_18003d6c0.md` and
cannot establish live-input parity.

## Capacity Contract And Observable Results

The entry predicate at `0x180042d28..0x180042d37` accepts only
`live_count < configured_maximum`. After successful insertion and order writes,
`0x180042e91..0x180042ea6` calls `FUN_18007a040(context,NULL)` and then
`FUN_180047550(context)` exactly when `live_count == configured_maximum`. For
profile 9/type 12 the configured maximum is 40. No graph, relation, or active
predicate intervenes, and both returns are ignored.

Insertion 40 is valid and commits before both finalizers. Insertion 41 returns
`0x80000005`, clears progress, and leaves the complete 40-feature object
unchanged. A graphless state is accepted at 40: closure leaves it graphless and
normalization writes inactive `bb=2288`. Sparse established-partial states can
gain closure edges and active markers before normalization; fully connected
states can remain packed-byte identical even though internal overlap counts are
recomputed. Exact closure and normalization mutations are owned by
`FUN_18007a040.md` and `FUN_180047550.md` respectively.

## Production Correspondence

Production `c182b140` limits `goodix_match_combine_templates()` to 40 inputs. At
exactly 40 it invokes `goodix_milan_relation_matrix_close()`, copies the resulting
active markers into packed `b5`, projects the post-closure reference star, and
runs the type-12 normalizer before returning the final bytes. Counts above 40
return `NULL` before reconstruction. This matches the DLL's successful count-40
finalizer order and count-41 no-result boundary while retaining native rollback
safety on helper failure.

The default adapter and libfprint enrollment paths still complete at 12 accepted
stages and do not naturally reach either capacity finalizer. Requested-8 evidence
is likewise below capacity.
