## Behavior

- Builds signed `setup - live` samples for packed subtype 12 and estimates a
  split from 15-row by 16-column blocks, excluding live samples at or below
  `499` from each block mean.
- Subtype 12 starts with span floor `1610` (70% of 2300). Large spans choose a
  midpoint from the appropriate side of 4095; small spans use
  `minimum_block_mean - 250`.
- It separates high/low sample populations around that split and adjusts the
  final threshold by one fifth or one quarter of their mean separation. If the
  low population exceeds 30% of the frame, it falls back to
  `minimum_block_mean + 150`.
- Eligible subtype-12 pixels must exceed the threshold and have live value
  below `3800`. The output structure stores the exact eligible-pixel count at
  offset zero, the mask bytes at offset `0x10`, the threshold at `+0x0c`, and
  integer coverage percent at `+0x0e`.
- The stored count is consumed by `FUN_180067500` before the separate
  rendering-mask postprocessing path. Profile-9 calibration update admission
  is initially tested at 80%, then packed subtype 12 overrides it with the
  strict condition `pixel_count * 9 < admitted_count * 10`; pixels added by
  later hole fill do not contribute.
