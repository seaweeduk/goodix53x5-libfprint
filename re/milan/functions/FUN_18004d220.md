## Recovered Behavior

It builds a one-pixel-padded signed-16 integral image while counting global zero
pixels, then writes clipped square-window zero counts for every source pixel.
The neighborhood radius is caller-selected. At the profile-9/type-12
`FUN_18004db10` call, decoded high classes below four use radius two; high
classes four or five use radius five. Type 11 substitutes radius seven in the
latter branch, but that path is outside profile 9.

## Confidence And Unresolved

- Confidence: high for zero-pixel polarity, integral dimensions, clipping, and
  output count semantics.
- Unresolved: overflow behavior on dimensions larger than the observed sensor.
