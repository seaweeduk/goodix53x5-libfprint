## Behavior

The function clones the live feature's row-packed matrix at `+0x140` into a
temporary working matrix and iterates the ordered candidate indices at context
`+0x87e8`, with count at context `+0x1c`. A record is `0x134` bytes (`77`
signed words). It is eligible only when word 1 (`+0x04`) exceeds four and word 5
(`+0x14`) exceeds 189. For each eligible record it:

- applies the record transform to derive a clipped coverage workspace through
  `FUN_180071370`;
- subtracts the transformed region through `FUN_180072600`;
- measures newly uncovered area with `FUN_180047580`;
- accumulates area-weighted record words 5 and 8; and
- retains the feature index with the greatest candidate count.

When context `+0x0c` is nonzero, the working dimensions and transform
translations are arithmetic-shifted right by one, while each newly uncovered
area is multiplied by four. Linear transform terms are unchanged. Processing is
ordered and destructive: each transformed region is removed from the same
working matrix before the next candidate, so increments are marginal rather
than independent overlap areas.

Eligibility and winner selection do not require a positive marginal area. Every
eligible record increments the eligible count and adds word 1 to the count sum,
even when its transformed region removes no remaining bits. Such a record can
become the strict-greatest-count winner; its zero area contributes nothing to
the weighted word-5 and word-8 sums or the large-increment count. The average
count is an unweighted average over eligible records, not an area-weighted
average. The incoming direct score is read only for the stronger transform
publication condition, before the rescue score replaces it.

Both the initial and subsequent remaining-area measurements use
`FUN_180047580`, which returns zero for a popcount below 20. A marginal area is
the difference of these thresholded remaining counts, not a separately
thresholded popcount of the removed region. On common-gate failure this helper
leaves the caller's score and all match evidence unchanged.

The ordered list is the lifecycle-order vector at context `+0x87e8`; unlike the
fallback finalizer, rescue does not scan physical feature slots. Area,
area-weighted products, total area, count sum, and their additions are signed
32-bit operations in the DLL. `IMUL` and `ADD` wrap at dword width, and the
subsequent `IDIV` operations truncate signed quotients toward zero. Half-
resolution area scaling is also a dword multiply.

For ordinary type-12 inputs, the 104-by-88 dimensions become a 52-by-44
rescue mask. `FUN_180073950` clears the final partial byte before packing its
four valid cells, leaving the upper four padding bits zero. Thus the initial
popcount is at most 2288. Destructive removal and the monotone below-20
threshold make marginal areas nonnegative and telescoping: their scaled sum
is at most `4 * 2288 = 9152`, regardless of contributor order.

Eligible word 5 is in `190..256`: `FUN_1800717e0` derives the detail ratio
from nonnegative cell-bin counts through `FUN_180073540`, with its optional
correction only decreasing the value; `FUN_180071d40` and `FUN_180072860`
publish that ratio. Word 8 is either its initialized zero or the low-bitmap
ratio written by `FUN_180070c70` / `FUN_18006fc80`, in `0..256`. It is not
the coverage value at word 9. Consequently each weighted sum and each of its
nonnegative prefixes is at most `9152 * 256 = 2342912`.

The type-12 branch of `FUN_18005a3c0` and `FUN_180057900` selects pair capacity
42. `FUN_18005b900` counts only surviving entries within that capacity;
secondary count promotion stays within it, and the topology bonus is clamped
to the same capacity. The caller merges retained and secondary counts by
maximum. With at most 40 ordinary gallery features, the eligible count sum is
at most `40 * 42 = 1680`, and the final score numerator is at most 4200.
These sums and products do not overflow signed dword arithmetic in this domain.

The common gate is exactly:

```text
eligible_count > 1
total_uncovered_area != 0
weighted_word5 / total_uncovered_area > 208
sum_count / eligible_count > 6
weighted_word8 / total_uncovered_area > 183
count(new_area > area_threshold) > 1
```

`area_threshold` is `1100` for types 11 and 21 and `1400` otherwise. Types
12, 13, 16, and 22 (mask `0x413000`) use score denominator 42; all others use
31. Every division is signed integer truncation and every comparison above is
strict.

When the common gate succeeds, the function writes evidence `+0x684 = 1`,
stores the strict-greatest-count feature at `+0x648`, and writes
`score = best_count * 100 / denominator`. Equal counts retain the earlier
ordered feature. It sets `+0x688 = 1` and copies that record's unscaled transform
to `+0x650..+0x660` only when the selected index is valid and at least one
strong condition holds:

```text
incoming_score > 40
or count(new_area > area_threshold) > 2
or (type in {11,21} and weighted_word5 / area > 216
                    and weighted_word8 / area > 202)
```

The ordinary acceptance write is independent of this stronger branch. Thus a
rescue may select an index and score while retaining the caller's prior
transform and leaving rejection evidence clear.

it does not reset the selected affine. The admission at

### Weak-Rescue Consumers

For type 12, an accepted rescue that omits the affine write leaves evidence
`+0x688` zero: admission required zero, the weak branch does not set it, and
the caller can only clear it afterward. `templateStudy` passes the saved
evidence to `FUN_180044fc0`. That dispatcher calls `FUN_1800469f0`, the
consumer of selected index `+0x648` and selected affine `+0x650`, only when
`+0x688` is nonzero. Weak rescue therefore cannot enter that selector, copy
the selected record's anti-fake fields into the probe, or use the selected
affine for the capacity-replacement footprint check.

The dispatcher is not otherwise a no-op. Its optional `FUN_180046430` prepass
uses retained-entry transforms from the evidence array and the independent
reference affine at `+0x66c`; it does not read `+0x650`. Capacity queue cleanup
and acceptance-controlled ordering/tail updates consume the gallery and their
own evidence fields, not the selected affine. The primary action remains zero
and the replacement index remains minus one on this branch. These ungated
operations do not make the preserved selected affine observable.

`identifyImage` publishes the positive score and gallery-array position, not
the feature-level selected affine. Its ordinary matcher call supplies a null
optional-result pointer, so the caller's optional affine copy is not exported
there. The selected affine remains in the saved evidence rather than in the
gallery's packed representation; the next matcher invocation clears that
evidence before selecting new geometry.

## Lifecycle And Relation Ownership

This rescue never increments feature `be`. A positive rescue keeps score
nonzero and bypasses both compact finalization and `FUN_18005d5f0`. It
preserves any earlier direct increments without adding a selected-feature
or contributor increment.

The strong branch at
`0x18005dd4b..0x18005dd7f` writes only `+0x688`, `+0x648`, and
`+0x650..+0x660`; the common success block at `0x18005dd88..0x18005dda3` writes
only `+0x684`, `+0x648`, and score. There is no access to relation count
`+0x668` or relation affine `+0x66c..+0x680`. Therefore relation evidence is a
separate caller-owned state:
the helper neither creates it nor clears it.

## Late-Status Row Ownership

The caller retains a completed 77-word candidate in the physical rescue table
before a nonzero `FUN_180058700` status continues traversal. The status result
prevents contribution, direct publication, and retained evidence; it does not
zero or omit that candidate's rescue row.
