descriptor accumulator consumed by `FUN_180048980`. It cannot add or remove
records and is downstream of the low-count retry.

The profile-9/type-12 extraction caller supplies descriptor scale `0x1adfe`;
this function multiplies it by three to `0x509fa`. Radius is symmetric
nearest-integer Q16 `((scale3 * 0x38916) >> 16)`, then capped only above at 32.

sample square is `dx,dy=-18..18` before image clipping. Candidate sampling clips
each bound to image interior `x=1..width-2`, `y=1..height-2`; loops are inclusive
and an empty clipped interval contributes nothing. The production candidate is
already in the six-pixel extrema interior, so every pixel address is valid.

`FUN_18004aaf0` visits the upper triangle `0 <= y <= x < size` and mirrors every
result. All radius squares, `power=(x*x+y*y)*coefficient`, polynomial products,
and stores are unsigned dword arithmetic. The coefficient is the low dword of
signed `0x200000000000 / (scale3*scale3)`. A power above `0x6ee75` yields zero;
otherwise the exact source polynomial at `materialization.c:30-44` is used with
unsigned truncating `/5`, `/6`, and `/0xc00` and logical shifts. Power zero
yields the maximum weight 512, and the approximation is at least `0x200`, so
the final divisor is nonzero on the reached domain.

At `0x1800491f2..0x180049213`, this caller sign-extends the complete 16-bit
orientation domain into `ECX` and calls `FUN_1800745a0`. For signed dword input

`0x1800745af..0x1800745fb`. On the descriptor's exact generic domain, let
