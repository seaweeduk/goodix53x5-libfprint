## Caller Admission And Ownership

`FUN_180055a40:0x180056d7a..0x180056d9e` requires score zero, accumulated
decoded high class below four, independent live primary-histogram class below
two, and configuration word 13 nonzero before entering the remaining no-direct
finalization paths. The histogram comes from the first byte expanded by
`FUN_180073830`; it is neither decoded packed low class nor the separate
`FUN_180058700` status counter. Earlier successful compact finalization exits
before the call to this fallback.

The no-live-class compatibility path substitutes decoded low for histogram;
its histogram gate therefore excludes positive-low fallback. Object-aware
recognition has independent histogram and packed-low owners. Queued recognition
uses packed mode zero and disabled feature mode, excluding its nonzero-low
fallback arm.

The caller owns one enable word and one 31-pair workspace per physical feature.
Failed direct count/topology admission can enable that workspace before the
separate admitted-transform proximity check at `0x180055f3c`. That direct check
therefore does not subsume this function's check.

The caller traverses lifecycle order `gallery+0x87e8`, whose values are physical
indices. At `0x180056fc9..0x180056ff2`, it stores the enable word and workspace
using that physical index, with a workspace stride of 62 dwords. This function
advances feature pointers, enable words and pair workspaces together at
`0x18005d8a7..0x18005d8be`, scanning physical slots in ascending order, not
lifecycle order.

If live probe count at `+0xf0` is zero, write score zero and return `0x80000006`.
Otherwise all rejection accumulators are initialized locally on entry:
`geometry_seen=0`, `count_sum=0`, `best_adjusted_detail=0`, winner feature `-1`,
and a zeroed `0x134`-byte candidate. Staged caller workspaces alone do not
initialize or publish these accumulators. A caller that bypasses this function
must not evaluate its rejection formula from the direct candidate.

## Decoded Proximity Mode

At `0x18005d6ad..0x18005d6da`, decode configuration word 18 through
`FUN_180070d90`, compare its two signed outputs, and use
`max(decoded_low, decoded_high)`. The signed `CMOVG` is at `0x18005d6d6`.
The mode is retained independently of the gallery's accumulated high class.
Current corresponding inputs are the decoded configuration high field and
`GoodixMilanMatcherLateContext.probe_low_class`.

Ordinary raw high fields `0..5` decode to `0,1,2,4,5,5`. Accumulation cannot
lower the initial high class, so caller admission excludes raw high 3..5.
Raw low `0,1,2,3` decodes to `0,2,2,3`. Thus modes 4 and 5 cannot reach this
proximity call on the ordinary admitted boundary.

For type 12, `FUN_1800624c0` modes 1, 2 and 3 use limits
`[25,21,1792,21,25,1792]` around identity `[256,0,0,0,256,0]`. Where each
difference and its negation are representable as signed dwords, return one
means every absolute difference is within its inclusive limit. Exact wrapping
arithmetic, including the `INT32_MIN` case, belongs to
`FUN_1800624c0.md` (Type-12 Table And Exact Arithmetic); caller admission does
not establish that representability bound. Equality at a limit is rejected by
this caller. Mode zero returns zero without a comparison. The mode-three
multiplier belongs only to the primary sensor-family loop; type 12's alternate
loop does not use it. A larger decoded low than positive high cannot change
the limits at this boundary. A positive low with high zero changes mode-zero
bypass into this inclusive proximity veto.

These arguments are distinct from direct admission: `FUN_180055a40:0x180055f3c`
uses decoded probe low alone after retained count `>4` and topology `>=30`.
The final processed metadata, including low-field preservation across high-mode

## Compact Vector And Score

If any candidate passes the overlap/detail gate, recompute only the winner's
low-bitmap metrics through `FUN_180070c70 -> FUN_18006fc80`, then recompute its
affine penalties with `FUN_180070e90`. The low-bitmap helper return is ignored;
unavailable intermediate images leave the initialized low words zero. It does
not restore raw detail or replace the count, coverage or affine.

The complete 15-word prefix passed to `FUN_1800608b0` at `0x18005d94e` is:

| Words | Owner/value |
|---|---|
| 0,1 | Winning filtered count |
| 2,3 | Zero |
| 4 | Winning overlap score |
| 5 | Winning penalty-adjusted detail, stored at `0x18005d880` |
| 6,7,8 | Recomputed low-bitmap metrics for retained feature/affine |
| 9 | Winning scaled coverage |
| 10,11 | Zero |
| 12 | Unsigned `average_scale - 234 > 47` |
| 13 | Signed `absolute_dot_q16 >= 0x147b` |
| 14 | Signed `absolute_dot_q16 >= 0x28f6` |

Type-12 construction writes configuration 16 zero and 17 one, preserved through
ordinary matching. Therefore stored adjusted detail equals raw detail here,
but the final compact penalties remain active. Equal words 0/1 exclude the
compact scorer's primary-less-than-retained adjustment. Its positive gate is
count `>=8`, scaled coverage `>=96`, and
`detail + low_detail - 4*(word12+word13+word14) >=417`. Its score is:

```text
q = (21 + filtered_count * 256) / 42
score = (q * 100) >> 8
```

Ordinary accepted counts `8..31` produce `19..73`. The caller caps positive
helper results at 100. A rank tie preserves count, adjusted detail and coverage
but can retain a different affine, low detail and final penalties: two tied
features need not agree on the compact gate. Physical tie order is therefore
consumed even though the winner index and affine are not directly published.

When compact scoring is not positive, write:

```text
-(((count_sum < 6) << 2) |
  ((geometry_seen == 1 && best_adjusted_detail < 208) << 1) |
  (geometry_seen == 1 && winner_coverage < 128))
```

The comparisons at `0x18005d978..0x18005d9b9` are strict; detail 208, count
sum 6 and coverage 128 clear their respective bits. When no geometry candidate
is seen, only the count bit is set. A nonnull optional diagnostic receives winner
coverage at `+0x20` (`0x18005d953..0x18005d95f`) on success or rejection, zero
without a winner. The normal return status is zero; the signed score is a
separate output.

## Publication And Lifetime

This function writes no lifecycle counter. At `FUN_180055a40:0x1800570ea`,
positive score sets acceptance evidence and exits directly at `0x1800570f5`.
Nonpositive fallback also exits without revisiting an increment loop; positive
fallback adds no increment and preserves earlier direct lifecycle mutations.
Selected feature `evidence+0x648` remains `-1`, with no direct/relation affine
publication from this retained fallback winner. The after-match gallery is not
rewritten by fallback selection. Absent selected feature makes a subsequent
study attempt action zero. Nonpositive fallback can be overridden by the
caller's blocking state to `-65536`; the outer `FUN_18005edb0` does not remap
this function's score.
