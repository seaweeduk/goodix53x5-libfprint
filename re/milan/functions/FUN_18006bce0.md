## Behavior

- Argument 1 is the normalized live u16 plane. Argument 5 is packed sensor
  type and argument 6 is the mutable mask structure; arguments 2 through 4 do
  not affect this helper. Rows/columns are signed dwords at `+4/+8`, count is
  the dword at `+0`, percentage is the word at `+0x0e`, and mask bytes begin at
  `+0x10`. Threshold at `+0x0c` is preserved.
- The type-12 caller supplies the complete clone of `FUN_18006a4c0`'s freshly
  initialized structure. Its 88 by 108 active mask contains only zero and one.
  `FUN_18006d540` copies `0x4ca0` bytes before the call at `0x18006d933`; the
  original structure is not an alias of the clone.
- Computes word row and column occupancy sums before changing the mask
  (`0x18006bd53..0x18006be22`). Neither summary is subsequently read to decide
  a fill. On production binary masks their maxima are 108 and 88, respectively.
- Zeroes the clone's count, then traverses row-major, rows and columns both
  ascending (`0x18006be40..0x18006bfc4`). For each zero byte, independently scan
  up, down, left, and right on the same row/column to the nearest currently
  nonzero byte or the image boundary. Searches include index zero and the last
  row/column. Missing up/left endpoints have sentinel zero; missing down/right
  endpoints have sentinels `rows`/`columns`.
- With those nearest endpoints `up/down/left/right`, write one exactly when
  `(up>=2 && down<=rows-3) || (left>=2 && right<=columns-3)`
  (`0x18006bf4e..0x18006bf88`). For production the endpoint limits are rows
  2..85 and columns 2..105. An occupied endpoint at row/column 0 or 1, or in
  either last two positions, blocks that axis; the search does not skip it to
  seek another endpoint. Either axis alone suffices, and no explicit gap-length,
  diagonal-support, or occupancy-percentage condition is applied. The fixed
  endpoint limits permit at most 82 intervening zeros vertically or 102
  horizontally in one bounded run.
- Mutation is in place: a fill is visible to all later pixel searches in the
  same pass. There is no snapshot, second fill pass, or convergence loop. An
  already nonzero byte remains unchanged during filling. Recount every
  nonzero byte after its turn, including preserved original ones
  (`0x18006bf8e..0x18006bf96`).
- Only after the complete fill/recount pass, type 12 clears each byte whose
  unsigned normalized live sample is `<=100` or `>=3800`. Decrement count only
  when clearing a currently nonzero byte. A subsequently cleared byte can thus
  have served as a fill endpoint earlier; clearing does not undo other fills
  or rerun morphology (`0x18006bfd8..0x18006c053`).
- Store the final `count*100/(rows*columns)` using signed `IDIV` into `+0x0e`,
  return zero, and leave dimensions/threshold and inactive mask tail unchanged
  (`0x18006c055..0x18006c095`). The active output is exactly 9,504 binary bytes,
  count `0..9504`, and percentage `0..100`; all count arithmetic fits a signed
  dword. The temporary summary arrays contain 256 words each, so the fixed
  production geometry is within their capacity. This is not a general contract
  for arbitrary helper dimensions or arbitrary nonbinary input masks.
- The related current owner is the postprocessing portion of
  `goodix_milan_profile9_build_contrast_mask` in
  `milan/preprocess/classification.c`. Its admitted-pixel output is captured
