derives two percentages from the enrollment progress word, updates the global
mode-1 policy counters, and asks `FUN_180042740` to classify the current stage.

output is nonzero, it sets status `4`, calls `FUN_180037a30` to remove the last
live feature, and decrements `state_dword[0x10]`. This rollback is independent
of the mode-0 adapter rollback in `FUN_18002d4b0`.
