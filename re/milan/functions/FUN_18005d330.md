## Configuration Lifetime

The follow-up-status output is initialized to zero at `0x18005d36b`.
`FUN_18005e230` runs once at `0x18005d3e4`, with the supplied matcher flags and
mode one. Its nonzero return exits this helper without scanning; the status
output remains zero. The dispatcher ignores this function's return value and
tests only the separate follow-up status for equality to five.

The configuration is one stack object at `RSP+0x60 == RBP-0xa0`, reused across
every occupied slot and continuation item. Word 13 (`+0x34`) is zeroed once
before the loops at `0x18005d413`. Word 14 (`+0x38`) is overwritten with the
current continuation index before each occupied-slot matcher call at
`0x18005d472`. The independent comparison copy of that trigger is retained at
`RSP+0x40`.

Matcher instructions `0x180055d80..0x180055d89` give these fields their exact
queued meaning: with word 13 zero, every gallery feature whose physical index
does not equal word 14 is skipped before feature `c7` decoding, correspondence,
policy, lifecycle, or evidence mutation. A queued rematch therefore evaluates
only its current continuation trigger; it is not a full-gallery comparison.

Word 18 (`+0x48`) is forced to zero at `0x18005d3ec`. Neither this function nor
`FUN_18005e230` initializes word 19 (`+0x4c`): its first value is preexisting
stack content, and subsequent calls share any matcher mutation of it. This is
not the ordinary caller's probe-`c7`/recognition-one initialization. See
`FUN_18005e230.md` for the initializer's field boundary.

Let `S` be the dispatcher's stack pointer at its primary-selector and consumer
call sites. This consumer's stack pointer is `S-0x7e0`, placing policy word 19
at `S-0x734` and incoming evidence `+0x690` at `S-0xa0`. The preceding primary
selector's stack pointer is `S-0x8d0`; those addresses overlap its local scratch
at offsets `+0x19c` and `+0x830`, respectively. Its append path does not write
that scratch, and its callees' ordinary stack frames lie below it. Neither word
therefore has a defined initializer supplied by the primary-append/consumer
call chain. Their values are residual stack bytes, not persisted queue fields
or values inherited from the original matcher policy.

For type 12, the policy dispatcher `FUN_180053220` reads word 19 only for its
late-eligibility call: the match flag must still be zero and word 19 must equal
one. The earlier ordinary classifier and veto sequence can return without that
read affecting flags. There is no word-19 reset on queued matcher entry, and
zeroing its separate evidence object does not initialize the policy word four
bytes before that object. A claimed constant initial recognition mode therefore
does not follow from either caller's stack setup.

Live queued `Q+0x150` remains a separate input. Matcher instructions
`0x180055c9e..0x180055cd5` decode that field into the initial low/high class
before gallery traversal even though configuration word 18 is zero. Word 18 is
consumed later by policy helpers, not by this initial class decode. Thus forcing
word 18 to zero does not make native queued matching discard Q's `c7`; it
changes only policy reached after a correspondence candidate is materialized.

The local matcher evidence starts at `RBP-0x50`; the caller does not memset that
object. It passes the address to the ordinary matcher, whose evidence setup
owns initialization. The score output at `RSP+0x44` is set to zero for each
call. The queued call supplies a null optional matcher-result destination as its
final argument; that argument is not the blocker seed. Matcher entry at
`0x180055a85` reads evidence `+0x690` before clearing the evidence at
`0x180055b3d`. This consumer does not initialize that incoming word either, so
null final argument alone does not establish a zero blocker seed.

A strong/direct candidate does not bypass this seed's consumer. After candidate
contribution, `0x18005667e..0x1800566e3` requires nonnull registration blocks,
both coverages above 40, and nonzero saved incoming `+0x690` before entering
`FUN_18003a3e0` and the blocker predicates. At `0x180056794..0x1800567a2`, one
type-12 rejection term is simply signed gallery-minus-Q `+0x12c8 > 415` and
`+0x1ab0 > 318`; it does not test weak geometry or a weak match flag. Rejection
branches to `0x180056c22` before direct evidence and gallery `be` publication.
That branch increments the blocker count, accumulates its score, and advances
to the next gallery slot. With only the trigger eligible, there is no second
candidate: rescue requires more than one contributor at `0x180056c75`, and the
queued mode suppresses terminal fallback. A blocked sole candidate therefore

increment the primary append's `be`, enter queued selection, and expose action

the incoming blocker seed.

Conversely, all three residual inputs are output-neutral when the trigger fails
before `FUN_180053220`: words 18/19 have no earlier consumer, and blocker seed

Identical transformed record arrays do not imply equal registration scalars:
Q was copied before primary selector entry, while that selector copies four

Thus the primary append's four scalars may differ from Q even when their record

does not remove this first-rematch dependency.

## Traversal And Consumption

Queue owner pointers start at gallery `+0x8d10`, and signed ranks at `+0x8db0`.
Each continuation item scans physical slots `0..19`, not rank order. Exact rank
`-1` skips a slot (`0x18005d466`); occupied producer ranks are nonnegative.

The continuation is a 20-dword stack ring at `RBP+0x650`. Slot zero receives
the primary selected index; the read cursor starts at zero and the write cursor
at one. At `0x18005d430..0x18005d45f` the helper reads the trigger and advances
the read cursor modulo 20 before scanning. There is no pending-count field or
packed continuation counter.

At `0x18005d4b3`, `FUN_180055a40` receives the queued live owner as probe and
the same already-mutated gallery as template. Only evidence `+0x688 != 0`
(`RBP+0x638`, tested at `0x18005d4b8`) enters the selector. This is the action
gate, not the score output or finalization gate `+0x684`. The selector receives
the same owner/gallery/evidence and replacement mode from configuration word 5.

For type 12, selector return `<= -1` leaves the entry and its rank untouched.
A nonnegative return executes `0x18005d4fb..0x18005d572` in this order:

1. Set the separate follow-up status to five.
2. Read the consumed rank and decrement all ranks strictly greater than it.
3. Set the consumed slot's rank to `-1`.
4. Write the selected gallery index at the ring write cursor and advance it
   modulo 20.
5. Break this physical scan if the returned index equals the saved trigger;
   otherwise continue to the next physical slot.

After the scan, equal read/write cursors terminate the helper; unequal cursors
start a new physical scan from slot zero. Twenty consumptions during one scan
can make the cursors equal with newly written ring entries unvisited, but all
twenty queue slots are then empty. Consumption neither inserts entries nor
clears/frees their owner bodies. Those independent preallocated owners survive
with rank `-1` for reuse until shutdown or gallery destruction.

An empty queue or no successful selector leaves status zero. Once status five
is set on the type-12 path it remains five. The ordinary return after scanning
is zero. A rejected rematch or failed selector does not roll back prior matcher
writes, primary mutations, or earlier queued mutations.

## Live Object And Finalization

The queue input is a full, independently owned live feature, not a packed
one-feature reconstruction. Type-12 enqueue copies clear record dword `+0x24`
and qword `+0x30`; the queued owner and a later appended/replaced gallery owner
remain separate allocations. Copy implementation belongs to `FUN_180037690`
and the insertion owner, not this consumer.

The same live gallery is passed to matcher and selector at `0x18005d4b3` and
`0x18005d4e8`. Thus matcher lifecycle writes remain visible even when its
selector returns `-1`; only a successful selector consumes the queue entry.
Gallery traversal uses the current stored order at `+0x87e8`, independently of
queue-slot order and physical ownership of newly installed live records.

The selector is called directly: there is no dispatcher retained-refresh or
full-capacity normalization prepass. Selector-owned normalization for a
capacity-reaching append or actions 3/4 is immediate and visible to later
rematches; below-capacity append and action 2 do not add that normalization.
The live per-feature overlap vector therefore survives until a selector-owned
normalization replaces it. See `FUN_1800469f0.md` for mutation contracts.

The direct queued call also does not repeat the dispatcher's quality/coverage
gate. The selector's own replacement quality predicate remains in force, but
below-capacity append has no extra outer quality/coverage test here. With a
nonnegative matched index and both registration blocks present, selector entry
copies matched-gallery registration dwords `+0x12c4/+0x12c8/+0x1ab0/+0x1ab4`
into Q before append/replacement admission. A later failed selector can therefore
leave a still-occupied Q with changed registration scalars. Consumption is based
on the returned selected index, not on whether any probe or gallery write took
place.

This helper performs no packing, final order generation, transaction-tail
increment, or queue shutdown. After it returns, the dispatcher may supersede
the primary action with five, performs exact-capacity shutdown, and only then
applies the original evidence's one-time accepted order/tail finalization.
Empty/no-positive continuation preserves the positive primary action; it does
not create an external action-zero family. See `FUN_180044fc0.md` and
`templateStudy.md` for dispatch and export ownership.

Queue bodies/ranks and the continuation ring are transient, absent from packed
templates. The type-12 helper never reads or writes serialized queue counter
`fb/+0x8e04`. Individual matcher scratch matrices expire before selector entry;
they are not queue or continuation state. The policy object, in contrast,
survives every rematch in this invocation.

## One-Entry Producer And Consumer Consequences

Fresh adapter gallery unpack initializes all ranks to `-1`, and an ordinary
match-time producer can insert at most one owner, at physical slot zero/rank
zero. Insertion requires quality `>15` and coverage `>65`, and the deep copy
preserves those fields. Those producer invariants also apply if a direct DLL
caller retains that live handle across the intervening action-zero study and
later reaches this consumer. Primary study mutates the independent original
probe and gallery, not Q. The consumer passes Q directly to matching; matching
reads its quality/coverage without replacing or preprocessing those scalars.

Thus a consumer invocation reached from that retained one-entry state has
exactly one occupied-slot rematch.
Failure appends no ring item: after the first physical scan the read and write
cursors both equal one, so retained Q is not retried. Success consumes Q before
adding a ring item; any subsequent scan is empty. Configuration mutations cannot
carry into a second occupied rematch on this path. The four registration-scalar
writes on selector failure likewise cannot feed a retry. Late dispatcher
insertion cannot change this: it requires primary index `-1`, whereas consumer
entry requires a nonnegative primary index. The ordinary adapter never reaches
this retained chronology because its action-zero cleanup ends Q's lifetime.

The queue copy leaves histogram owner `Q+0x158` null. Matcher instructions
`0x180055cc7..0x180055d08` initialize the three histogram words to zero, skip
`FUN_180073830` on that null pointer, and pass first word zero as the independent
histogram class. It is not recovered from Q's copied `c7`.
Naturally admitted Q can carry `c7` `0x000`, `0x100`, `0x200`, or `0x300`, and
the matcher decodes that live field independently of policy word 18. No constant
initial accumulated-high value follows from the null histogram owner. Queued
word 13 zero separately suppresses post-loop fallback/aggregate finalization;
it does not suppress the trigger's direct correspondence, policy, blocker, or
selector paths.
