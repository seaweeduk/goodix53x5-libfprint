# FUN_18006b510

## Identity

- Binary: `GoodixEngineAdapter.dll` 2.0.310.900.
- Address/body: `0x18006b510..0x18006b98f`, profile contrast renderer.

## Call Graph

- Callers: primary live rendering and `FUN_18006d430` refined rendering.
- Substantive callees: `FUN_18006cbc0`, `FUN_180069e80`, `FUN_18006eaa0`,
  `FUN_18006cd30`, and allocation helpers.

## Behavior

- Type 0's coating-enabled `FUN_18006cbc0` path is a direct source copy;
  type 12 applies `FUN_180065dd0`, `FUN_180065bf0`, and the coating-clear
  filter. Low-range diagnostics use threshold 50 for type 0 versus 150 for
  type 12. Diagnostic `+0x4fb4` is bin 199 of the histogram at `+0x4c98`:
  it is cleared by the `0x320`-byte histogram memset and incremented for
  included samples whose range is at least 199. The later coating helper
  `FUN_180069e00` consumes that bin, not a separate uninitialized count.
  See [the coating contract](../PROFILE0-ENGINE-CONTRACT.md#final-quality-and-coating-rejection)
  for histogram fields, allocation ownership, and rejection predicates.

- Applies the profile pre-contrast transform, derives local min/max planes,
  merges their ranges, and emits inverse 8-bit contrast under the rendering
  mask. It optionally records low-range diagnostics.
- `FUN_18006cd30` expands the merged bounds with the center and four in-bounds
  diagonal neighbors. The final lower bound is the maximum of their merged
  minima, and the final upper bound is the minimum of their merged maxima.
  Equal bounds render zero; an excluded mask sample renders `0xff`; otherwise
  the function truncates `(source - lower) * 255 / (upper - lower)`, clamps it
  to `0..255`, and subtracts it from 255.
- On the profile-9 live path, `FUN_18006d540` supplies workspace `+0x1cb64` as
  this function's output. That primary contrast plane is later consumed by
  `FUN_1800392f0` even when preprocessing selects a different refined plane for
  the exported image.
- The output loop writes all `width * height` bytes, including the entire first
  row. Output storage's previous contents do not supply any pixel or metadata
  field. The loop has no first-row exception and does not clear, reserve, or
  encode any output low bits. Excluded pixels are explicitly written as 255;
  they are not left at their caller-initialized value.
- At `0x18006b83a..0x18006b845`, the numerator is the signed difference of
  zero-extended 16-bit prepared source and lower bound, multiplied by 255;
  signed division truncates toward zero. The clamped quotient is inverted at
  byte publication. This is full-byte intensity quantization, not a parity or
  metadata normalization step.
- `FUN_18006d430` also calls this renderer after preparing the refined source.
  `FUN_18006e7f0` copies either complete rendered plane to the selected image,
  without changing its pixels. First-row metadata interpretation and encoding
  occur later; see `FUN_180070fc0.md` for its conditional prefix initialization.
