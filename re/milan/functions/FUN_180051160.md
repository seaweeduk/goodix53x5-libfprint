## Flood Contract

`FUN_180051f50` (`0x180051f50..0x180052373`) is the shared score-admission flood;
its current counterpart is `milan_profile9_flood()`. Arguments are the signed
word admission plane, signed word threshold, mutable byte mask, columns, and
rows. For the 88x108 temporal plane, all linear indices and coordinates fit
the helper's signed-word queue entries.

- Normalize every existing nonzero mask byte to 1, including border bytes
  (`0x1800520d0..0x1800520e0`).
- Visit interior seeds in row-major order. Only bytes still equal to 1 start a
  traversal. Enqueue the seed without relabeling it or testing its admission.
- Process the queue FIFO. A dequeued outer-border pixel is retained but cannot
  grow neighbors (`0x1800521cd..0x1800521ee`).
- Neighbor order is up-left, up, up-right, left, right, down-left, down,
  down-right. A zero mask neighbor is admitted exactly when its signed word is
  greater than or equal to the signed threshold (`0x180052210..0x180052254`).
  Relabel it 2 before enqueueing. Existing seeds are not traversed through that
  enqueue branch, but each interior seed gets its own later traversal.
- Border neighbors can be admitted, but a preexisting border seed never starts
  traversal. Relabeling on insertion prevents duplicate growth entries; each
  traversal contains its seed plus at most all initially zero pixels.
- Normalize all remaining nonzero bytes to 255 and return zero. The admission
  plane is unchanged. Three temporary arrays hold word indices, columns, and
  rows, each allocated with 0x9920 bytes and freed before return. Allocation
  results are used without a local failure branch.

The resulting mask is the union of the original seeds and threshold-admitted
eight-connected growth from interior seeds, with outer-border pixels terminal.
There is no priority sorting, direction-plane traversal, valid-mask check, or
invalid-region fill inside this flood. In the temporal caller unqualified pixels
have score zero and the grow threshold is at least 700, so they cannot be added.
The flood affects the class plane but not the secondary histogram state or its
fallback guard.
