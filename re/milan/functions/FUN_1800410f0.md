## Contract

The input points at the presence byte, not the enclosing tag. A zero presence
byte stores a null output owner, decrements the caller's remaining-byte count by
one, and returns the next byte. A nonzero presence byte reads a little-endian
dword length, allocates exactly that many bytes, copies the payload, decrements
the remaining-byte count by `5 + length`, and returns the first byte after the
payload.

This leaf is generic and does not itself require length `0x1abc` or validate the
remaining-byte count before allocation/copy. At its profile-9 `bf` call site,
valid completed probes supply presence one and length `0x1abc`, making it the
decode-side inverse of `FUN_180040840` for the canonical anti-fake block.

Malformed lengths and allocation-failure behavior are outside the valid
profile-9 serialization contract.
