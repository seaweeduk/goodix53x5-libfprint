## Alternative Construction

The local context contains the source feature, incoming affine pointer,
overlap-mode descriptor, retained-count context, map radius, two lazily allocated
record-index maps, and coverage scale. At `0x1800728b5..0x180072931`, the maps
are constructed once for the feature's two stored partitions. The split is
feature `+0x118`; the second record range starts at `records + split*0x38`.
Record foreground bytes do not reconstruct the partition boundary.

`FUN_180073ba0`, called at `0x180072950`, collects inverse-affine spatial pairs
through these two maps, appends the second partition's pairs, and rebases both
indexes by their stored partition starts. At `0x180073cb4..0x180073cc1`, it
copies the incoming affine into the alternative output. It calls similarity
fit `FUN_1800732c0` only when the combined pair count is at least three
(`0x180073cc6..0x180073ce4`). Fewer pairs leave the copied affine unchanged.
This pair count is not published as the candidate's retained count.

## Type-12 Publication

At `0x180072991..0x1800729bf`, `FUN_180071d40` evaluates the alternative with
null optional mismatch and masked-reclassification outputs. It supplies selected
overlap score, selected detail, and primary coverage. Publication is exactly:

```text
if alternative_overlap == 128: leave candidate and selected affine unchanged
if incumbent_overlap != 128 and alternative_detail <= incumbent_detail:
    leave candidate and selected affine unchanged
otherwise:
    publish alternative overlap, detail, and scaled coverage
    recompute transformed-record outputs using unchanged candidate word 1
    publish the alternative affine
```

The sentinel test is incumbent overlap word 4, not detail word 5
(`0x180072a17`). The strict detail comparison is at `0x180072a24..0x180072a27`.
Tied detail does not replace a nonsentinel incumbent. Type-12 coverage is
`(primary_coverage * context_scale) >> 8` at `0x180072a43..0x180072a4f`.

Writes are candidate words 4, 5, 9, 10, 11 and 66..76, plus the six-word affine
output. `FUN_180074e80` clears all eleven words 66..76; only 66..70 can be
nonzero for type 12. Words 10/11 copy words 68/69. Candidate words 0..2 and
12..14 are not modified. In particular, this helper does not recompute the
affine penalties for the replacement transform. Words 15..20 are materialized
by the caller after return.

For a nonsentinel incumbent, publication makes detail strictly greater but
places no monotonicity requirement on overlap score, coverage, topology, or
geometric percentage. No raw image quality, raw image coverage, recognition
mode, or policy flag is used as a publication predicate in this helper.

## Record And Pixel Domains

The call at `0x180072a69..0x180072a91` supplies unchanged candidate word one
as the retained-count argument to `FUN_180074e80`. Let `N` be that count,
`V` the number of transformed records inside the destination border, and `M`
the number with a same-low-flag spatial neighbor. The outputs at
`0x1800751f7..0x180075211` are:

```text
m10 = V > 0 ? trunc(100*M/V) : 0
m11 = V > 0 ? trunc(100*N/V) : 0
```

`V` counts records, not bitmap cells. For ordinary probes,
`0 <= M <= V <= probe.record_count <= 150`. The spatial scan admits transformed
Q8 positions in `[6,width-7)` and `[6,height-7)`, compares record flag low two
bits, and requires nearest-neighbor squared distance strictly below `0x40000`.
Descriptor distance contributes to the average-distance output after a spatial
hit; it is not an additional type-12 match-count acceptance threshold.

For nonzero `V`, topology `34..50` is exactly
`34*V <= 100*M < 51*V`. Neither this ratio nor the unchanged retained-count
numerator is a bitmap-coverage ratio. Bitmap shared-support counts belong to
`FUN_1800717e0` and its mask classification, as described in `FUN_1800572e0.md`.

## Ownership And Lifetime

Feature records, stored partitions and source bitmap owners are read-only.
The two allocated index maps are retained in the caller's local refinement
context for reuse; caller cleanup releases them after traversal. The alternative
affine and overlap scalars are local until publication. A losing or tied
alternative leaves the caller's selected state intact.
