## Behavior

This is the watershed propagation stage for the anti-fake class map. It forces
the outer border to `0xfe`, discovers unassigned interior pixels adjacent to an
assigned four-neighbor class, and queues them by the minimum absolute source-
image difference. The queue is processed in ascending difference order.

An unassigned pixel inherits a common nonzero four-neighbor class. Conflicting
neighbor classes become `0xff`; queued-but-unresolved pixels use `0xfd` while
pending. Newly exposed four-neighbors are enqueued with their absolute image
difference, preserving the 0..255 bucket order.
