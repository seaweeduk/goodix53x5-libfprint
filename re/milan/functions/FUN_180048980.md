It normalizes and thresholds descriptor accumulators, then dispatches the packed
descriptor layout selected by the caller mode. It is downstream of candidate
count selection.

therefore reads four unsigned dwords per group; reached counts 128 and 32 are
exact multiples of four. Each unsigned dword is squared to 64 bits and energy
addition wraps modulo `2^64`. On the reached domain, `FUN_180074980` returns
unsigned floor `sqrt(energy)`; threshold is the low dword of
`(root*0x3333)>>16`.
`FUN_180074940` returns unsigned floor square root for each dword. Native compares
the sample and threshold as unsigned, storing `sqrt(sample)` only when sample is
strictly below threshold and otherwise storing `sqrt(threshold)`; every result
narrows to signed 16 bits. With the sampler bound `M=11,213,479`, energy is at
most `128*M*M < 2^54`, so neither energy addition nor the threshold product
wraps. The root is below `2^27`, keeping the dword doubling at
`0x1800749a0` exact. Every normalized value is nonnegative and at most
`floor(sqrt(M))=3348`; this also bounds the independently normalized central
