## Behavior

- Initializes three full-resolution gain maps to Q13 unity (`0x2000`) and
  clears the application map for a fresh generation.
- For the production mode-bit-clear path, selects update threshold 3 and scalar
  controls 8000/6400 unless the packed subtype is 4 or 11.
- Profile-9 subtype 12 therefore fixes the optional reciprocal-plane numerator
  used by `FUN_1800668f0` to `8000 * 8192`; it is not the binary's initial 2000
  value before generation initialization.
- Once the sample counter exceeds the selected threshold, normalizes the
  application map by its rounded full-frame mean and marks initialization
  complete.
- The persistent ready scalar at RVA `0x1efbfc` is not written here. This helper
  owns Q13 map initialization and the update-sample/state scalars; the ready
  transitions occur later in `FUN_180067500`. Treating nonzero calibration-map
  samples as a ready substitute is therefore incorrect.
- Fresh-generation map/counter reset at `0x1800673cf..0x180067413` does not own
  or clear ready. The profile scalar therefore crosses every call that causes
  this initializer to rebuild generation-local planes.
- `FUN_18006c510` temporarily forces the workspace sample count to 5 around its
  post-render `FUN_1800668f0` call, then restores the saved count. Internal map
  arithmetic observes 5, but the increment does not persist.
