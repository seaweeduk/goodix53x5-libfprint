### Type-12 Spatial And Ratio Contract

The enrolled feature supplies destination width and height at offsets 0 and 4,
record count at `+0xf0`, and its borrowed 56-byte record array at `+0xf8`.
The probe supplies its own count and record array at the same offsets. Coordinates
at record offsets 2 and 4 are loaded as unsigned 16-bit Q8 values. The affine
products and translation shifted by eight use signed 64-bit intermediates;
division by 256 rounds half away from zero before narrowing to a dword
(`0x180074f40..0x180074fdb`).

A transformed probe record is valid exactly inside the inclusive lower border
`6*256` and exclusive upper borders `(width-7)*256`, `(height-7)*256`.
For each valid probe, the enrolled scan compares only flag bits `0..1` and
requires each absolute coordinate difference to be at most 512. It keeps the
first strictly nearest record, retaining the earlier physical index on a
squared-distance tie (`0x180075021..0x180075082`). A hit requires squared
distance strictly below 262144. Enrolled hits are not consumed: multiple probe
records may select the same enrolled record.

For type 12 a hit always increments matched count. Its distance contribution
is the smaller Hamming distance between enrolled bytes `40..47` and probe bytes
`40..47` or `48..55`; there is no descriptor-distance rejection after the spatial
hit. The sum is divided by matched count with signed truncation, or remains zero
without hits. With valid count `V`, matched count `M`, and caller count `N`,
the percentages are `trunc(100*M/V)` and `trunc(100*N/V)`, both zero if `V=0`
(`0x1800751d5..0x180075211`). Only the first ratio is bounded by 100: `N` belongs
to the caller's correspondence stage, is not recalculated from this spatial
scan, and the second ratio is not clamped. Neither ratio uses bitmap support
or raw image coverage as its denominator.

It forward-transforms probe records, rejects points outside a six-pixel image border, finds an enrolled record with matching low flag bits within a small spatial radius, and accumulates descriptor distance. Outputs include valid transformed count, matched count, topology percentage, geometric percentage, and average distance in words `66..70`; the special-type-only count at word 71 remains zero for type 12, and reserved words `72..76` remain zero.
