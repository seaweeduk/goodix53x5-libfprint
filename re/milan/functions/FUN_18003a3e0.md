## Behavior

The function compares the record arrays stored at the start of two anti-fake
objects. Counts come from `+0x12c0`; packed support masks start at `+0x12dc`.
It transforms second-object record coordinates, forms nearest-neighbor matches
within squared distance `0x400`, keeps mutual nearest pairs, calculates a
per-pair descriptor distance, and writes five aggregate words to the separate
output object.

The output is exactly five signed dwords at indices `0..4`. There are no words
5, 6, or beyond:

| Index | Producer | Valid profile-9 domain | Native consumers |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Never written; every caller zero-initializes it | exactly `0` | None |
| 1 | Mutual nearest-pair count | `0..100` | Blocker pair-count predicates in `FUN_180055a40`; no consumer in the other two callers |
| 2 | Rounded symmetric pair support, `(valid_total/2 + pairs*8192)/valid_total`, with zero-total result zero | `0..4096` | Blocker pair-coverage predicates in `FUN_180055a40` |
| 3 | Rounded directional valid-support ratio, `(first_valid/2 + second_valid*4096)/first_valid`; when `first_valid==0`, `second_valid*4096` | `0..409600` | Blocker support-ratio predicates in `FUN_180055a40` |
| 4 | Lower median descriptor distance from `FUN_180039c10` | `0..192` | Raw blocker predicates and adjusted blocker-sum evidence in `FUN_180055a40`; adjusted type-17 evidence in `FUN_180062a30`; running `+0x1ab4` update in `FUN_180041ac0` |

All words are nonnegative in valid objects even though callers load and compare
them as signed dwords. At `0x18003a8ed..0x18003a951`, a zero pair count skips
all stores to words 2 through 4, even when either valid-support count is nonzero.
Word 1 is always written at `0x18003a95e`; word 0 is never written. Thus a
no-pair result leaves all five words zero under the callers' zero-initialization
precondition, not because this helper clears the output. A pair result
writes words 1 through 4; word 0 remains the caller default. The return value is
zero exactly when word 1 is zero and one exactly when word 1 is nonzero.
`FUN_180041ac0` consumes this return alias before using word 4; the matcher
callers ignore it.

The descriptor distance is the sum of two bounded terms. The head term is the
minimum of two 64-bit Hamming counts (`0..64`). The tail term is
`tail24 + min(tail32 + tail40, 64 + tail44 - tail32)`. Here `tail24` and
`tail32` are in `0..64`, while `tail40` and `tail44` are in `0..32`.
The two alternatives share `tail32` with opposite signs: their sum is
`64 + tail40 + tail44 <= 128`, so their minimum is at most 64. Thus the
tail is at most 128 and the complete distance is at most 192, including after
lower-median selection. The bound is attained with head 64, `tail24=64`,
`tail32=32`, and `tail40=tail44=32`. The blocker therefore receives raw detail
in `0..192`, never the enrollment projection's no-pair sentinel `-1`.

Both anti-fake inputs are read-only in
the complete body. In particular, this function neither reads nor writes
anti-fake boundary score `+0x12d0`.

All affine and distance arithmetic is dword-width before aggregation.
`FUN_18003c520` obtains the inverse from `FUN_1800689a0`, whose determinant is
`wrap32(a*d - b*c)` and whose wrapped-zero case copies all six input words.
Forward record mapping at `0x18003a5c0..0x18003a602` uses dword `IMUL`/`ADD`,
then wrapping `+0x80` and arithmetic shift. At
`0x18003a690..0x18003a6ac`, coordinate deltas, both squares, their arithmetic
shifts by eight, and their sum are likewise dword operations before the
unsigned inclusive `0x400` comparison.

Second records are eligible only when their rounded forward coordinates are
in bounds and active in the first-domain intersection. Their nearest-first
search nevertheless visits every first record, including first records whose
own intersection bit is clear. The reverse search visits only first records
with an active intersection bit and uses the stored distances of eligible
second records; other matrix entries retain `UINT32_MAX`.

Both searches replace the best index only on a strictly smaller unsigned
distance (`0x18003a6b1..0x18003a6bc` and `0x18003a773..0x18003a77e`). Ties
therefore retain the lowest physical record index, not the lowest descriptor
distance or the next supported record. Both reject only distances greater than
`0x400`. Only reciprocal selections contribute descriptors, in first-record
order. Counts of supported records include records without reciprocal matches.

For nonzero pair count both support counts are positive. Their sum is at most
200 and each count at most 100, so the signed support numerators do not wrap.
The median helper selects signed rank `(pair_count - 1) >> 1`, giving the lower
median for even counts. It mutates only the temporary descriptor-distance array.

## Recognition Consumer

The type-12 matcher calls this function at `0x18005670e` only after the
candidate has contributed. Both anti-fake owners must be nonnull, both feature
coverages must be greater than 40, and the incoming blocker seed saved before
matcher evidence initialization must be nonzero. The six-word transform at
`RBP+0x218` is the final selected candidate affine, copied into candidate words
15 through 20 after optional refinement. The pair call therefore does not select
or reconstruct a second affine. High/enhanced metrics are refreshed on an
accepted refinement, and low metrics are computed later if dispatch requires
them. Masked-overlap counts retain the original admitted affine: refinement
does not request that descriptor. See `FUN_180055a40.md` and
`FUN_180072860.md` for this distinct ownership.

The caller consumes words 1 through 4 together with wrapping signed differences
of the two anti-fake objects' texture, mean, and threshold scalars and the
gallery object's retained pair score. The ladder uses raw word 4 (saved in ESI);
only the accumulated blocker sum uses its adjusted value (R14D). A blocker hit
at `0x180056c22` increments the invocation's blocker count and accumulates that
adjusted value, then skips direct, retained, relation, and lifecycle publication
for that feature. It does not roll back the earlier contribution, prefix sum,
contributor count, or ranked candidate replacement. Another feature can still
publish a positive direct score using a prefix that includes blocked contributors.
Thus a blocked feature
does not imply a rejected gallery.

After traversal, a nonzero blocker count disables aggregate rescue and compact
acceptance, but it does not itself forbid a positive fallback. Queue admission
at `0x180056d4b` precedes final rejection and has no blocker-count predicate:
it requires cleared rejection evidence, configuration word 13 equal to one,
probe low class zero, probe coverage greater than 65, quality greater than 15,
accumulated high class below four, probe histogram class below three, and gallery
queue state zero. A rejected result can therefore still leave a queued probe.
The final override predicate is `(count > 5 && sum > count*60) ||
(count > 10 && sum > count*50)`. It can replace a previously published score
with `-65536` without undoing selected-feature, affine, evidence, lifecycle, or
queue mutations. In the zero-score fallback branch, a positive fallback returns
before the blocker rejection; a nonpositive fallback becomes `-65536` when the
blocker count is nonzero.

At this matcher boundary, records and bitmaps produce the selected affine and
overlap metrics, while each feature's anti-fake pointer at `+0x160` owns pair
records, support mask, and persisted scalars. Separate storage does not make
these fields independent producer outputs. The matcher checks presence and
coverage, and passes the actual selected affine; it does not establish which
joint record, bitmap, mask, and scalar states extraction or template decoding
can supply. A nonidentity affine is passed unchanged when its candidate reaches
this call, rather than replaced with an identity comparison.

`drivers/goodix53x5/milan/match/match.c` maps the caller through

`feature_result->transform` produces the overlap metrics and candidate transform,

Ordinary matching supplies the live probe anti-fake object and enables this
