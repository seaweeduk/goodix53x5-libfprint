nested objects at offsets `+0x08`, `+0x10`, `+0x18`, `+0x20`, and `+0x140`, frees
buffers at `+0xf8`, `+0x100`, `+0x158`, and `+0x160`, frees the feature object,
and clears the caller's pointer. A null owner or null object returns
`0x80000002`.

`templateStudy` supplies the address of retained probe global `0x18024ebe8`
on its normal valid-gallery completion path and on its dispatcher-error path.
The final object free and owner clear are `0x180037c20..0x180037c28`.
Neither operation destroys the separately owned matched gallery at
`0x18024e548`. A late-enqueued deep copy remains owned by that gallery.

The probe is consumed once **when study runs**, not once per identify call.
`identifyImage` does not call this destructor on no-match or normal return;
its next extraction can reuse the same allocated probe. Adapter
`FUN_18002ba60` calls study only for its successful-match latch. Thus zero
action after a positive match still destroys the original probe, whereas a
no-match that never enters study leaves it allocated. See `identifyImage.md`
for the resulting allocation/reuse and zero-residual invariant.
