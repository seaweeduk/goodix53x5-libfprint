## Behavior

- The mode-zero quality-rerender wrapper `FUN_18006d190` also calls this helper
  with configuration rows/columns when signed render-mask coverage is at least
  96, then passes the centered words to `FUN_18006a1f0`. It shares the centering
  arithmetic with refined rendering; its byte renderer is a separate owner.

- Arguments are source u16 plane, destination word plane, row count, and
  contiguous column count. The type-12 mode-zero caller supplies 88 rows of
  108 words, with 216-byte row strides. See `FUN_18006d430.md`.
- Two 32-bit accumulators sum even and odd unsigned source words; an odd final
  column is added separately. Unsigned division by column count truncates the
  row mean. At 108 columns the sum cannot overflow for any u16 source.
- Each output uses 16-bit subtraction of the mean and addition of 5000 before
  signed-positive testing: `max((int16_t)(source - mean + 5000), 0)`.
  `0x18006b230..0x18006b248` performs the narrowing before the clamp.
- Every word participates, including positions later excluded by the rendering
  mask. This helper receives no mask. The source is read-only and every output
  word is written; production supplies a separate destination. No allocation or
  status output is owned here.
