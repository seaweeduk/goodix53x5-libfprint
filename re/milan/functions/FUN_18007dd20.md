## Recovered Behavior

It selects Gaussian tables by numeric ID range: IDs below 100 use table
`0x1800f2910`; `100..199` use `0x1800f2bd0`; `200..299` use
`0x1800f2d50`; and IDs from 300 use `0x1800f2f90`. Each entry is 64 bytes and
starts with coefficient count followed by signed 32-bit coefficients. The
100-wide dispatch ranges do not establish 100 valid entries per table; this
function does not validate the selected index against a table capacity.

The type-12 low-count retry consumes entries `100..105`. Their exact coefficients
are listed with ordinary entries `0..5` in `FUN_18004ac40.md`. These entries
occupy `0x1800f2910..0x1800f2a8f` and `0x1800f2bd0..0x1800f2d4f`.
Every one of those twelve entries sums to
`0x10000`. Entry `6` at `0x1800f2a90` is the separate pre-scale blur kernel
`[0x5555,0x5555,0x5555]`, sum `0xffff`.

ID `6` uses the uniform-sum execution selected by `FUN_18007be70`.
`FUN_18007de70` sums three unsigned words, multiplies once by `21845`, then
logically shifts by 16 into a dword. `FUN_18007e180` sums the central horizontal
result and its two neighbors, multiplies by `21845`, adds zero bias, logically
shifts by 16 and stores a word. Factoring out the common coefficient is exact:
the unweighted sum is at most `196605`, and its product is at most
`65535*65535 = 0xfffe0001`. Horizontal output is at most `65534`, vertical output
at most `65533`. This preserves the two separate truncations; it is not division
by 9 or a single final truncation. Reflection and state ownership are described
in `FUN_18007d800.md`.

`FUN_18004ae70` lifts bytes by 8 before ID `6`, then copies the high byte of
each filtered word into the scale source. Thus a constant byte image `c` in
`1..255` becomes constant `c-1`: the word passes give `256*c-1` then `256*c-2`.
A zero image stays zero. In particular, this preblur does not preserve a
constant 255 image as 255; its Q16 coefficient sum is one below unity.
