## Recovered Behavior

It enumerates triples lexicographically as `(first,second,third)`, with
`first=0..count-3`, `second=first+1..count-2`, and
`third=second+1..count-1`. The admitted-triangle counter is tested against
`0x3b2` only before each new `first` body and increments after all three edge
and orientation gates but before the solver. It is therefore a soft outer-loop
threshold, not a 946-solve cap. With the production maximum 42, the first two
outer bodies contain at most `C(41,2)+C(40,2)=820+780=1600` triples. If their
admitted total is still below 946, later bodies remain eligible. Any body with
first index at least two starts with at most 945 admissions and contains at
most `C(39,2)=741` further triples. Thus 1686 is a combinatorial upper bound on
admitted/solved triangles, without asserting that the geometric gates can attain
that bound. Rejection before admission can move the terminating body later.

The first pair's coordinates and normalized orientation are loaded in the
`first` body at `0x180077f9c..0x180077fe0`. The second pair's coordinates and
first-edge gate are evaluated in the `second` body at
`0x180078030..0x1800780c2`; failure advances `second` through
`0x180078643..0x180078673` without entering the `third` loop. On success the
second orientation is normalized once before the third-pair body at
`0x180078150`. These cached values remain immutable across third indices;
orientation retry writes a separate three-dword scratch array at
`0x180078200..0x18007822d`.

The first two edges compute dword `dx` and `dy`, wrap each dword square,
arithmetic-shift each square by two, and then wrap the dword addition. The third
`(second,third)` edge instead wraps both dword squares and their dword sum before
one arithmetic `>>2`. The first form is at `0x18007804e..0x180078089` and
`0x1800782b3..0x1800782d4`; the second is at
`0x180078327..0x180078340`. Each ratio gate then evaluates, in order,
`source*5 <= target*6`, `target*5 <= source*6`, `source > 0x2ffff`, and
`target > 0x2ffff`, branching at the first failure. All four products use
32-bit-destination `LEA`/`ADD`, discard carry modulo `2^32`, and are compared as
signed dwords by `JG`/`JL`; no overflow flag participates. The three ratio/gate
sites are `0x180078078..0x1800780c2`, `0x1800782d7..0x18007830c`, and
`0x180078340..0x180078379`. Both 5:6 equalities are inclusive. Within the
canonical sensor coordinate range, the exact distance threshold is not
representable by the first form: the nearest values are `0x2fffd` and
`0x30000`, so the latter is the first reachable passing magnitude.

For each selected pair, `enrolled_orientation-probe_orientation` is normalized
once: values strictly above the half-period subtract one period and values
strictly below its negative add one period; equality is unchanged. The three
dword differences are summed with dword arithmetic and divided by three with
signed truncation toward zero. Every deviation must be within inclusive
`[-tolerance,+tolerance]`. If that first test fails in mode 1, a local retry copy
adds the half-period to each original difference, applies the same one-step
normalization, recomputes the truncating average, and tests once more. Retry
state is local and never mutates caller orientations.

After `FUN_1800683d0`, the candidate must satisfy strict
`abs(a-d)<50`, `abs(c+b)<50`, and `abs(a),abs(b),abs(c),abs(d)<300` gates.
Thus 49 and 299 pass while 50 and 300 reject. Scoring forms each transformed
coordinate with signed 64-bit coefficient products and `+0x80` before `>>8`,
then performs target subtraction and translation addition as dwords. Absolute
dword components must be at most 640 (`<0x281`), and the wrapping dword sum of
dword squares must be strictly below `0x64000`. Component 640 already squares
to the rejected equality `0x64000`; component 641 fails both gates. `0x63fff`
is not a sum of two integer squares; the nearest reachable value below is
`0x63ffd`.

Candidates below argument-9 minimum inliers are discarded before residual,
ranking, or validation. Otherwise residual is `(sum+(count>>1))/count`, with
the zero-inlier sentinel `0x190000`. A candidate is eligible only for strictly
more inliers or equal inliers with strictly lower residual; exact ties preserve
the earlier triple. `FUN_180080130(affine,401,163)` runs only after that rank
test, so an invalid nominal winner preserves the earlier valid model. The
candidate affine is temporarily written to the supplied affine buffer before
ranking/validation, but common completion republishes the saved winner or six
zeros. A successful replacement atomically commits affine, residual, and the
42-byte mask.

Early exit tests the retained best count strictly above 20 after rank and
validity processing. Twenty continues searching; 21 exits immediately with the
first accepted 21-inlier model, so no later lower-residual transform is visited.

Profile-9 mode uses period `0x3244`, half-period `0x1922`, and orientation
tolerance `0x400`. Primary and alternate callers use minimum inlier count 2;
the type-12 fallback in `FUN_18005d5f0` passes 4 through argument 6 of
`FUN_18005b900`, with a 31-slot correspondence workspace. See that wrapper's
note for its separate compacted-input gate.
`FUN_180080130` validates the affine with bounds `401` and `163`.

On the unsigned-16-bit coordinate domain, a triangle passing the edge and
coefficient gates necessarily contributes at least two geometric inliers, before
orientation postfiltering. The two are source points 0 and 2, which anchor the
solver's X and Y translations respectively. This does not require image-sized
coordinate bounds. To account for quotient narrowing, let a row's exact Q10
coefficients be `A,B`, its truncated integer quotients be `qA,qB`, and their
narrowed dwords be `rA,rB`. Passing the coefficient gates bounds each `r` to
`[-1196,1199]`. For either independent source edge, the difference between the
target delta times1024 and `rA*dx+rB*dy` has magnitude at most
`65535*1024+1199*131070`; the quotient truncation error contributes less than
131070. Their sum is below `2^32`. Writing `q=r+k*2^32` therefore forces
`kA*dx+kB*dy=0` on both independent edges, hence both `k` values are zero.
Singular triangles already fail the coefficient gates.

Each quotient differs from its exact Q10 coefficient by less than one, and
the final coefficient shift discards a remainder in `[0,3]`. Translation floor
and transformed-coordinate rounding together add error of magnitude below1.5
Q8 coordinate units. At an anchor, an axis residual consequently has magnitude
below `3*131070/1024+1.5 < 386`. For the other axis at points 0 and 2, add at
most `(abs(dx)+abs(dy))/1024`. Their source edge passed the shift-each-square
gate: both squared dwords cannot be negative, since their shifted sum would
remain negative. At least one coordinate difference thus has magnitude at most
46340, giving `abs(dx)+abs(dy)<=65535+46340=111875`. The other-axis magnitude is
below496. Both anchors pass the axis limit641 and the strict squared limit:
`386^2+496^2=395012 < 409600`. The minimum2 gate cannot discard a triangle
that otherwise reaches scoring on this domain; the separate minimum4 fallback
gate does not share this implication.

The same coordinate and narrowed-Q10 bounds give translation magnitudes below
220000 and every pre-refinement mapped residual component magnitude below440000.
Thus the scoring subtraction/addition cannot wrap a signed dword or supply
`INT32_MIN` to its absolute-value gate. Components surviving the axis gate are
at most640; their squared sum is at most819200 and cannot wrap either. These
bounds apply before least-squares replacement, not to the later refined affine.

The sole triangle producer `FUN_1800683d0` narrows each quotient to a dword and
arithmetically shifts it right by two. Its linear coefficients are therefore in
`[-0x20000000,0x1fffffff]` even after quotient wrapping or a singular solve.
Neither a coefficient nor the symmetry sum/difference can be `INT32_MIN` or
overflow a signed dword (see the solver note). This producer bound is necessary:
the gate's signed negate-and-compare sequence alone would accept `INT32_MIN`.
The four strict coefficient gates precede residual ranking and the validator
call. Together with that producer bound, every recognition candidate reaching
`FUN_180080130` has
`abs(a),abs(b),abs(c),abs(d) <= 299`: each wrapped dword product is its ordinary
signed product, the two square sums are in `[0,178802]`, and the cross sum is in
`[-178802,178802]`. Sign-extending each product before the qword sum cannot
change recognition validity within this domain. The wider product-sum contract
remains material to enrollment `FUN_1800792d0`, which has no coefficient gate.

Argument 9 is the minimum accepted geometric-inlier count. Argument 10 is 1
on these wrapper calls and selects the period/half-period/tolerance above.
Entry initializes residual `0x190000`; the no-model epilogue writes six affine
zeros. It does **not** initialize the supplied
42-byte output mask on failure. Caller `FUN_18005b900` owns zero initialization
of that private mask before this call. A direct no-model call with poisoned mask
therefore preserves the poison, while the production wrapper observes mask zero.
Exact count/residual ties retain the earlier triangle.

## Failure Contract

The estimator has no return status. Its output families are:

- no accepted model: residual `0x190000`, six affine zeros, caller-owned mask;
- accepted model: fitted affine, accepted inlier mask, and rounded residual;
- early success: the same accepted outputs when best inlier count exceeds 20.

Degenerate solves, coefficient pre-gate rejection, `FUN_180080130` rejection,
and search exhaustion all converge on the same no-model output. The estimator
temporarily writes a candidate affine before the validator call, but if no model
is accepted the epilogue overwrites it with zero. Model validity must therefore
be carried explicitly by native code; neither all-zero, identity, residual, mask,
nor later retained count is a validity test.

Least-squares refinement runs after this validation and can publish coefficients
outside the triangle bounds. The refined affine is not validated again; its
subsequent owners are residual replacement, affine diagnostics, bitmap/record
consumers, and candidate ranking.

The primary/alternate profile-9/type-12 calls use count `3..42`, minimum inliers
2, and mode 1. The fallback uses the same mode with minimum inliers 4.
Count below three initializes residual and reaches the no-model affine-zero
epilogue without reading point arrays. A count above 42 is malformed: once any
triangle reaches scoring, mask index 42 calls the non-returning bounds handler at
`0x18007873d`. Null residual faults at entry; null affine faults at final
publication; point/orientation pointers fault only when their count-driven paths
read them. Production wrapper arguments exclude these native crash surfaces.

The corresponding current owner is
`goodix_milan_filter_recognition_pairs_internal` in `match/geometry.c`.
