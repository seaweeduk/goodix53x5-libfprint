## Live Publication

the earlier post-render and `FUN_18006b290` status candidates, but before its
late `0x7531` predicate and final return-status selection. An early
raw-validation `0x29aa` returns before the call and explicitly leaves both
metrics zero; setup-side `0x29bb` occurs before any live metric call. Pending or
late `0x7531` and severe `0xc351` status do not skip quality evaluation.

The caller selects `0x7531` or overriding `0xc351` first, then applies the signed
coverage comparison. Coverage `<=5` forces quality to zero, while coverage 6

the finalized image into the selected retained workspace. Status selection,
quality policy, and state commit are therefore independent in that exact order.

`metrics[1] = quality`, despite the internal quality-then-coverage argument
order. It also copies the final low bytes to result-descriptor `+0x28/+0x29`.
