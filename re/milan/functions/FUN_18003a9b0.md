## Behavior

The function builds a 4096-bin histogram from interior residual values below
`4095`, accumulates from the high end, and selects the first level whose upper
tail exceeds `rows * columns / 50`. It stores that level in `threshold_out`.

Pixels at least `threshold * 11 / 10` are then examined with a 3x3 neighborhood.
The nine roughness values come from `FUN_18003aff0`, which clamps each requested
center independently to interior `x/y` coordinates. When their lower median
from `FUN_180039c10` satisfies `median * 5 <= center * 2`, this function builds a
second nine-value set directly around the original flat residual pointer and
replaces the pixel with its lower median. This replacement load does not reuse
the clamped center: its offsets are exactly `[-columns-1, -columns,
-columns+1, -1, +1, +columns-1, +columns, +columns+1, 0]`. Interior column-zero
pixels therefore take their left neighbors from the preceding flat row, and
interior last-column pixels take their right neighbors from the following row.
Other pixels remain unchanged.

The direct load can leave the allocation for qualifying pixels sufficiently
near its first or last flat element. The current implementation models the
in-allocation flat loads exactly and retains a defined clamped fallback where
the shipped DLL would read outside the residual object; this is a separate
boundary condition from the canonical-zero feature-mask byte.
