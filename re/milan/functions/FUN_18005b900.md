## Source Ownership

The caller's complete pair workspace and both feature-record tables are read-only.
All removal occurs in compacted local index arrays, not in argument 7. The
42-byte private mask is initialized completely, so its unused tail remains zero
through estimation, orientation filtering, and refinement. Local coordinate and
orientation arrays preserve the caller's pair order. They are not rebuilt after
orientation removal: refinement scans the original compacted coordinates with
the post-orientation mask and can rescore pairs outside that mask.

The related current owner is
`goodix_milan_filter_recognition_pairs_internal` in `match/geometry.c`; its input
is the already compacted correspondence sequence. Native robust triangle

For mode 1, the estimator visits triangles in increasing first, second, then
third compacted index. Only triangles passing all edge and orientation gates
consume its `0x3b2` budget, before the three-point affine's coefficient checks.
The budget is checked only at the next first-index iteration; the remaining

An exact count/residual tie keeps the earlier triangle. An incumbent count above
20 terminates the robust search before wrapper orientation removal.
