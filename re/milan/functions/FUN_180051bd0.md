## Exact Contract

For every masked pixel, inspect the eight neighbors in this fixed order:

```text
left, right, up, down, up-left, down-left, up-right, down-right
```

Out-of-bounds or unmasked neighbors are skipped. Select the first neighbor with
a strictly largest positive `int16(center - neighbor)`. Write that positive
difference to the uint16 gradient plane and direction index 0..7 to the signed
direction plane. If there is no positive valid difference, write gradient 0 and
direction -1. Unmasked pixels also receive 0/-1.

The difference subtraction and comparison are word operations, with the
comparison signed (`0x180051d64..0x180051d7a`). Equal positive differences do not
replace the first selected direction. All output pixels are initialized before
the mask test, so an unqualified pixel cannot retain an old direction or gradient.
Coordinates and linear indices are narrowed to signed words; the 88x108
profile-9 plane fits those index operations.

The temporal consumer is [`FUN_180052b80.md`](FUN_180052b80.md). Direction edges
always connect two qualified, in-bounds pixels. For source values in 0..32767,
every selected edge is a genuine strict descent, and the positive gradient is
at most 32767. The current counterparts construct the pair directly in
`milan_profile9_temporal_class3()` and `milan_profile9_primary_path_scores()`.

The primary component consumer [`FUN_18004f540.md`](FUN_18004f540.md) receives
the gradient plane but not the direction plane. It thresholds the gradient
and retained age, then labels four-connected candidate pixels. Its component
edges are therefore not the selected descent edges: a selected eight-neighbor
descent may be diagonal or end outside the thresholded candidate set, while
two orthogonal candidates are component neighbors regardless of either
direction. Acyclic strict descent in a nonnegative signed-word source does not
make that component graph a tree or bound its breadth-first frontier.

On the initialized profile-9 primary path, the filtered prepared samples are
in `3000..11190`; subtraction cannot wrap, and gradients lie in `0..8190`.
Each nonempty eight-connected valid-mask component has a sample of minimum
filtered value. Such a sample has no lower valid neighbor and receives gradient
zero. Thus at least one valid sample is excluded from the positive-gradient
population, independently of direction tie order. The primary eroded support
has at most 6624 samples, giving at most 6623 positive gradients. See
`FUN_18004f540.md` for the setup/filter range derivation and resulting candidate
threshold/population bounds.
