## Behavior

- Samples every second row and column into a half-resolution persistent
  reference.
- Inputs are the u16 update-source plane, width, height, entry auxiliary-map
  count, coarse-reference pointer, and subtype. The comparison loads both
  current and reference words as signed 16-bit values. With normalized setup
  and live inputs in `0..4095`, subtype-12 signed-baseline source values are
  in `0..21285`, so those loads preserve their nonnegative values.
- The caller supplies its update-source plane directly, not the later Q13
  ratio plane. `FUN_180066690` changes the separate temporary gain plane;
  the caller allocates the ratio output for `FUN_180063a80` only after this
  gate has accepted.
- When prior state exists, computes the mean absolute difference from that
  reference. Production subtype 12 increments the stable counter when the mean
  is below 30; otherwise it resets the counter and refreshes the reference.
- With prior state, the integer mean divides by
  `floor(width / 2) * floor(height / 2)` across
  the entire coarse grid, with no mask-based exclusion or rounding addition.
  At 108x88 the divisor is 2376; a sum of 71280 therefore resets the stable
  counter, while any smaller nonnegative sum increments it.
- Production subtype 12 admits calibration while the stable counter is at most
  3. Other profile branches use different thresholds.
- An entry auxiliary count of zero refreshes the coarse reference and resets
  the stable counter to zero. Otherwise a mean difference at least 30 performs
  the same refresh/reset, while a smaller difference increments the counter
  without replacing the reference.
