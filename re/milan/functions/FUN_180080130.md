## Recovered Behavior

For affine linear part `[a,b;c,d]`, each scalar product is a wrapping signed
dword multiplication which is then sign-extended. It forms
`first=a*a+b*b`, `cross=a*c+b*d`, `second=c*c+d*d`, all as signed 64-bit sums,
then computes wrapping signed-64 `trace=first+second` and
`disc=trace*trace+4*(cross*cross-first*second)`. Both `disc` and `trace` must be
nonnegative.

The two integer arguments are shifted left nine as dwords before signed
extension. With the profile-9/type-12 call `(upper,lower)=(401,163)`, define
`lo=(lower<<9)-trace` and `hi=(upper<<9)-trace`. Acceptance is exactly
`lo <= 0`, `hi >= 0`, `disc < lo*lo`, and `disc < hi*hi`; both squared-bound
equalities reject. The products in those final comparisons are wrapping signed
64-bit. When the products and discriminant do not wrap, this is equivalent to
strict lower/upper characteristic limits, not inclusive limits. The upper
equality is reachable by ordinary integer linear parts and rejects. The nominal
lower equality is not realizable by an ordinary integer 2x2 linear part, but its
strict branch remains part of the wrapped-input contract.

The product/sign-extension/add sequence is at `0x1800801ab..0x1800801bb`;
the sign and strict-bound gates are at `0x1800801fb..0x180080231`.

There is no pointer or argument validation. A null affine faults. Arbitrary
large coefficients and bounds retain the wrapping dword/qword behavior above.
At `0x1800801ab..0x1800801bb`, each product is narrowed and sign-extended
separately before the qword addition; the sum is not narrowed to a dword.
Recognition supplies coefficient-filtered candidates, but enrollment supplies
every nominally better triangle without a coefficient bound. Nearly singular
enrollment triangles can therefore reach this validator with large coefficients
even when their input coordinates are bounded unsigned words. Both callers use
fixed `401/163` bounds.

Recognition `FUN_180077e20` calls this function only after strict
`abs(a),abs(b),abs(c),abs(d) < 300` gates. Its sole triangle producer
`FUN_1800683d0` publishes dword quotients after arithmetic `>>2`, bounding all
four coefficients to `[-0x20000000,0x1fffffff]`. This excludes `INT32_MIN`
and overflow in the preceding symmetry sum/difference, so the signed absolute
value gates establish genuine magnitudes at most299. In that domain each product
has magnitude at most `89401`; the two square sums lie in `[0,178802]` and the cross
sum in `[-178802,178802]`. Consequently the per-product sign extension cannot
change any recognition validator result. Recognition least-squares refinement
runs later and is not passed back through this function. Enrollment
`FUN_1800792d0` has no coefficient gate and is the profile-9 path on which the
full signed-qword sum width can change model acceptance.

`first=-4294865347`, `cross=-211`, `second=89042`; trace is
