  const uint16_t *calibration,

## Behavior

For every interior pixel it forms a 32-bit temporary value:

```text
calibration[p] - raw[p] - sensor_offset - 0x1bb7
```

The low 16 bits are copied into the dense residual output. For chip types `12`
and `17`, the first/last rows and first/last columns are replicated from their
nearest interior neighbors. The supplied mask is scanned while finding
temporary min/max values, but those values are not returned and do not alter the
residual output in this body.

## Ownership And Ordering

The temporary allocation contains `rows*columns` dwords and is initially zero.
The interior subtraction uses dword arithmetic at `0x18003a210..0x18003a22a`;
the full-plane copy at `0x18003a273..0x18003a29e` writes every destination word,
including zero temporary borders, by taking each dword's low word.
Type-12 replication then copies the first and last rows before copying the
first and last columns. Consequently all four corners acquire the diagonally
nearest interior sample. The calibration, raw and mask owners remain immutable;
the temporary is freed on return. Successful allocation and disjoint writable
output are the normal caller preconditions; no status or allocation-failure
handling is provided.

`FUN_1800392f0` supplies workspace map `+0x9924`, the live raw frame, geometry
`88x108`, and the same sensor-offset operand used by its caller. The complete
residual is subsequently mutated by `FUN_18003a9b0`, then consumed together
with the dense morphology mask by `FUN_18003b0b0`. See those owning notes for
the filtering and ordered-candidate contracts.

## Setup and Raw Provenance

For ordinary type-12 setup, `FUN_180064bb0` writes normalized setup sample
`S + 7095` at workspace `+0x9924`. Consequently this function's interior
residual reduces exactly to `u16(S - L - sensor_offset)`, where `L` is the
original live raw sample supplied to the anti-fake builder. The adaptive
calibration/application-gain maps are not operands of this subtraction.

`FUN_180069820` clamps setup samples to `0..4095` and copies only the outermost
rows/columns on the validated type-12 route. It leaves the inner boundary
rows1/86 and columns1/106 unchanged, even for0/4095 values. Raw admission in
`FUN_1800695e0` counts only the two-pixel interior; it does not constrain those
inner-boundary samples. A valid raw12 live sample and clamped setup sample
therefore allow `S-L` throughout `-4095..4095` at those positions.

The residual's own edge copy duplicates each inner-boundary result into its
outer neighbor; it does not average away an isolated inner-boundary impulse.
The resulting residual can have equal row0/row1 impulse pixels despite valid
setup/raw admission. A later mask does not alter this residual producer. See
`FUN_18003a9b0.md` for why duplicated edges alone do not determine every
allocation-end replacement median.

### Inner-Edge Underflow And Rendering-Mask Admission

The residual's unsigned narrowing has a discontinuity that the upstream
signed-difference mask does not share. With normalized setup `S=3000` and
sensor offset zero, live values 3000 and 3001 produce residual 0 and 65535,
respectively, while `FUN_18006a4c0` sees setup-minus-live 7095 and 7094.
Both live values satisfy the type-12 raw range and later rendering-mask
range checks.

More precisely, take an 88x108 normalized plane whose other residuals are in
`[951,1049]` and put either value at row 1, column 52. Border replication changes
exactly row 0, column 52 as well. The normal setup-minus-live values are then
`[8046,8144]`. The changed 15x16 block contains 238 normal samples and two
samples 7094 or 7095, so its mean is at least 8038. Every block mean is at
most 8144. `FUN_18006a4c0` therefore takes its `span<=1610` arm and selects a
split in `[7788,7894]`. The phase comparison uses only rows 24..87 and its
difference is at most 98. Exactly 9502 samples enter the high population and
two enter the low population; the resulting threshold lies in `[7526,7657]`.
Exactly those two changed pixels are initially excluded. The 30% fallback
does not run, and the original 9502 count passes type 12's strict 90% normal
calibration coverage gate.

`FUN_18006bce0` fills both excluded pixels using horizontal endpoints 51 and 53.
All normalized live values are strictly between 100 and 3800, so its later
range-clearing pass removes none. The actual rendering-mask clone is thus
all one for both sides of this residual-underflow boundary. This establishes
raw admission, normal calibration coverage admission, and rendering-mask
support together; it does not equate that clone with the later selected-image
gradient mask. The gain update, rendering, candidate selection, post-render
and classification stages precede that latter mask and retain their own
predicates. See `FUN_18006a4c0.md` and `FUN_18006bce0.md` for mask ownership
and the distinct pre-fill and post-fill counts.
