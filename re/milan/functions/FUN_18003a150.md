
## Ownership And Ordering

The temporary allocation contains `rows*columns` dwords and is initially zero.
The interior subtraction uses dword arithmetic at `0x18003a210..0x18003a22a`;
the full-plane copy at `0x18003a273..0x18003a29e` writes every destination word,
including zero temporary borders, by taking each dword's low word.

first and last columns. Consequently all four corners acquire the diagonally
nearest interior sample. The calibration, raw and mask owners remain immutable;
the temporary is freed on return. Successful allocation and disjoint writable
output are the normal caller preconditions; no status or allocation-failure
handling is provided.
