## Count And Graph Reconciliation

surplus feature owners, rewrite remaining feature ordinals, restore the order
prefix, and reconcile graph state. It reads `f2/+0x87d8` and `f5/+0x87e4`, not

last active prefix feature. The path does not provide a general range check

## Record Canonicalization

After limit and optional count reconciliation, type 12 visits every retained
56-byte record. A negative signed orientation at record `+6` receives one
addition of `0x3244`; nonnegative orientations are unchanged. Each record then
passes through `FUN_180047ae0(record, 0)`, reversing the packed descriptor
pair/nibble layout installed by `FUN_18003d6c0`. The transform preserves
`+0x20..+0x23` and zeros the omitted dword `+0x24..+0x27`; mode zero does not
construct the alternate descriptor tail at `+0x30..+0x37`.

Foreground and vertical class have already been reconstructed by the feature
decoder. This loop neither derives them again nor edits the packed scalar tags.
See `FUN_18003d6c0.md` for their sources and `FUN_18004c9f0.md` for the inverse
descriptor operation used during serialization. The enclosing decoder next
calls `FUN_180047550` to rebuild residual coverage from masks and relations.

`FUN_18004bbb0` produces angles in `[0,0x3244)`, and the final quantizer in
`FUN_180049b90` at `0x18004a570..0x18004a5a4` maps them to nonnegative multiples
of `0x100` in `[0,0x3200]`. `FUN_180040a70` consequently emits orientation
bytes `0..0x32`, which decode without sign conversion. The bounded record-copy
helper `FUN_180037690`, used for append and replacement, copies the orientation
word without geometric transformation; its type-12 suffix clearing cannot
introduce a negative orientation. Relation-affine installation is a separate
owner and does not rotate these persisted record words.
