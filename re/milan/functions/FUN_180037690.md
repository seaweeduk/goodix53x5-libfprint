## Recovered Behavior

The helper allocates destination record storage when needed. If the source count
exceeds the configured limit, it sorts compact `(strength,index)` entries and
copies the selected records; otherwise it copies the complete `0x38`-byte record
array. It updates the destination count and derived record state.

When its fourth argument is nonzero, it clears a dword at record offset `+0x24`
and a qword at `+0x30` for every copied record. In profile 9/type 12, both queue
`P->Q` through `FUN_180045a50` and append `P->G[p]` through `FUN_1800456b0` call
with nonzero transform mode. The resulting Q and G[p] record arrays therefore
clear bytes `36..39` and `48..55`; neither destination preserves those source-P
spans, and each destination owns its transformed copy independently. The helper
receives only record-array state and does not read or write live feature optional
`c7` at `+0x150`; it therefore does not control the append omission directly.

offsets `+24..+27` and `+36..+43`, which are whole-record offsets `+0x24` and
