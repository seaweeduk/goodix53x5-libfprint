```text

|| d-configuration[0] > Bp[N]+4 || o-configuration[0] > Bn[N]+4
```

Match is assigned candidate AND raw quality at least 16 AND raw coverage at
least 65 AND candidate coverage `m9 >= 40` AND:

```text

|| d > Sp[clamp_signed(n,0,21)]+5 || o > Sn[clamp_signed(n,0,21)]+5
```

All threshold comparisons are signed and strict as written. Broad count-shift
configuration does not shift the strict table indexes. Both outputs are

temporary results into its flags.
