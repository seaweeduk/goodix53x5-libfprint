## Packet Contract

- Requires workspace signature `0x24cc7bf4f728f6f4` at `+0x26488` and version
  `0x2711` at `+0x26490`.
- Imports history-plane count at `+0x26494`, component-age count at `+0x26498`,
  and reference/support count at `+0x2649c`.
- Each word at `+0x264a0` packs three two-bit history values, four-bit component
  age, and six-bit support age.
- A 44x54 reference plane follows the 9,504 packed words and is bilinearly
  reconstructed to 88x108.
- Import ends after that reference at packet-relative `+0x5ce8`. The remaining
  `0x4318` bytes in the persisted `0xa000` block are not packet fields and are
  not read by this function.
- Imported scalar counts are trusted. Valid native exports cap history planes at
  3, component ages/count at 5, and support ages/reference count at 50.
- Workspace `+0x26488` is the address `DAT_18023faf8`. The official calibration
  serializer copies `0xa000` bytes from this address to payload `+0x1848c`, and
  the loader restores them before `preprocessor_init`. The packet is therefore
  retained across attachments, not merely across live calls in one workspace.
