## Behavior

- The mode-zero quality-rerender wrapper `FUN_18006d190` also calls this helper
  with configuration rows/columns when signed render-mask coverage is below 96,
  then passes the centered words to `FUN_18006a1f0`. It shares the centering
  arithmetic with refined rendering; its byte renderer is a separate owner.

- Arguments are source u16 plane, destination word plane, byte mask, row count,
  and contiguous column count. The type-12 `FUN_18006d430` mode-zero call passes
  88 rows and 108 columns; row strides are 216 source/destination bytes and 108
  mask bytes. See `FUN_18006d430.md` for caller mode and coverage provenance.
- For each row, nonzero mask bytes admit unsigned 16-bit source words to a
  32-bit sum and increment a 16-bit contributor count. The unsigned division
  truncates; no contributors gives mean zero. With 108 columns neither the sum
  nor count can overflow, even across the full u16 input domain.
- Every excluded destination word becomes 5000, including an entirely excluded
  row. Every included word is computed by 16-bit subtract-mean and add-5000,
  then interpreted as signed 16-bit and replaced with zero unless positive.
  Thus the exact operation is `max((int16_t)(source - mean + 5000), 0)`, not
  an unrestricted 32-bit clamp followed by narrowing. Arithmetic/store sequence:
  `0x1800697c8..0x1800697e5`.
- The source and mask are read-only. All destination words are written for
  positive dimensions; the production wrapper owns a distinct destination.
  There are no allocations or status outputs in this helper.
