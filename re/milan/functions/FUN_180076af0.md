## Recovered Behavior

It first compacts every nonzero mask slot into fixed 42-entry local arrays. Each
source row is `[x,y,0x100]`; target is `[X,Y]`. Coordinates are signed dwords in
this direct helper and are unsigned-16-bit values when materialized by
`FUN_18005b900`. It accumulates the 3x3 normal matrix and 3x2 right side with
wrapping signed 64-bit multiply/add. Every cell is then normalized independently
as wrapping `(cell+0x80)>>8`.

The nine adjugate cofactors and all later dot products use wrapping signed
64-bit arithmetic. It computes the determinant from the first matrix row,
`rounded=determinant+0x80`, `denominator=rounded>>8`, and
`half=rounded>>9`. Denominator zero returns immediately without writing the
supplied affine. Otherwise each coefficient numerator uses the corresponding
adjugate row. A nonnegative numerator is signed-`IDIV` divided as
`(numerator+half)/denominator`; a negative numerator is
`-((half-numerator)/denominator)`. Division truncates toward zero and each result
narrows to dword. The six solved words are rearranged into
`[a,b,tx,c,d,ty]`.

The dividend addition/subtraction wraps before signed interpretation and `IDIV`
at `0x180076f4f..0x180076f65`. A nonnegative numerator close to `INT64_MAX`
can produce a negative `numerator+half` dividend and a negative quotient.
Likewise, `half-numerator` can wrap negative for a numerator close to
`INT64_MIN`, even when the denominator is
positive. The final negation then produces a positive quotient. Positivity of
the Gram determinant alone does not permit replacing these signed divisions
with unsigned-magnitude division. Publication still depends on the subsequent
all-pair residual and retained-count checks.

It then rescans all supplied pairs, not only original mask members. Each mapped
coordinate uses signed 64-bit products and `+0x80` before `>>8`, followed by
dword target subtraction and translation addition. Dword residual components
are squared into qwords and accepted only when their unsigned qword sum is
strictly below `0x64000`; there are no separate component limits in this helper.
The mean is unsigned `(sum+(count>>1))/count`, or `0x190000` for no retained
pairs. The complete refined affine replaces the input only when this mean is
strictly below the supplied robust residual and refined retained count is at
least the number of nonzero input-mask bytes. Equal residual, fewer retained
pairs, denominator zero, and all other failures are no-write outcomes. The
helper never updates the caller's mask, count, or residual.

## Output Ownership

Both complete coordinate arrays and the full input mask remain unchanged. The
normal equations use only nonzero mask entries, but the replacement predicate
counts geometric inliers across every supplied pair, including pairs removed by
orientation filtering. It does not reapply orientation filtering or
`FUN_180080130` affine validity to the refined model. No output is published
until both the strict residual improvement and nondecreasing inlier-count
conditions pass. The current corresponding owner is
`milan_refine_affine_least_squares` in `match/geometry.c`.

For the 16-aligned Q8 coordinate domain inside a 104-by-88 image, normal-matrix
normalization is exact: each product is divisible by 256. The matrix remains a
Gram matrix. With `n <= 42`, its nonnegative determinant is bounded by
`n^3 * (104*256)^2 * (88*256)^2 / 4096`, at most
`6506985026645655552`. This follows by centering both coordinate columns,
bounding each variance by one quarter of its squared range, and dropping the
nonnegative squared covariance term. The bound plus `0x80` is below `INT64_MAX`;
wrapping intermediate cofactor products therefore recover the same final
determinant, and rounding cannot produce a negative denominator on this domain.
This bound concerns the determinant, not the separately wrapped coefficient
numerators.

## Failure Contract

This helper has no status and commits only the complete six-word refined affine.
The wrapper neither changes its retained count nor publishes refinement status.
It has fixed local capacity for 42 compacted points but no entry validation:
malformed positive counts can overrun locals, and null mask/coordinate pointers
fault on count-driven reads. Nonpositive count or fewer than three usable
noncollinear points converges to denominator zero and no write. Production calls
are bounded to 42 and occur only for post-orientation retained count `>3` and
robust residual `>0x4000`.

## Published Coefficient Domain

The triangle coefficient bounds do not bound this function's published affine.
Orientation filtering can remove the three noncollinear points which supplied
the robust model while retaining four points on two almost coincident parallel
lines. The remaining normal equations can be nonsingular but ill-conditioned.
No triangle-consistency or affine-validity predicate is reapplied to the solution.
