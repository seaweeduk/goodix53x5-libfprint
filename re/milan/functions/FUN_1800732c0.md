## Fixed-Point Equations

Pairs are `[enrolled_index, probe_index]`; probe coordinates are the source
`(x,y)`, enrolled coordinates the target `(u,v)`. All coordinates are unsigned
16-bit Q8 values at record offsets 2 and 4; record stride is `0x38`.
Coordinate sums accumulate in 32-bit registers and the squared, dot and cross
sums in 64-bit registers. Let `n` be the pair count, and define the signed
arithmetic-shift reductions below. Additions and products wrap at their register
width; the coordinate reductions sign-extend the 32-bit sums after adding 128.

```text
X = (sum(x)+128)>>8; Y = (sum(y)+128)>>8
U = (sum(u)+128)>>8; V = (sum(v)+128)>>8
S = (sum(x*x+y*y)+32768)>>16
D = (sum(u*x+v*y)+32768)>>16
C = (sum(-u*y+v*x)+32768)>>16
H = X*X+Y*Y
den = n*S-H
```

When `den == 0`, there are no output writes. Otherwise signed divisions truncate
toward zero and the remaining right shifts are arithmetic. The final negation of
`c` is 32-bit:

```text
r = ((den>>1)+0x800000000)/den
k = (r*H+0x800000000)/n
a = ((n*D-V*Y-U*X)*r)>>27
c = ((n*C-V*X+U*Y)*r)>>27
tx = (-r*X*D+r*Y*C+k*U)>>27
ty = (-r*X*C-r*Y*D+k*V)>>27
output = [low32(a), -low32(c), low32(tx), low32(c), low32(a), low32(ty)]
```

There is no coefficient-validity check or residual-improvement gate here.
`FUN_180073ba0` supplies at least three ordered spatial pairs and preinitializes
the affine; `FUN_180072860` owns subsequent overlap-based selection.

The four coordinate accumulators are dwords (`ADD EAX/R14D/R15D/R13D` in
`0x18007333f..0x180073378`). The squared, dot and cross accumulators are
qwords. The cross term negates the zero-extended source Y as a dword and
sign-extends it before multiplication (`0x1800733ad..0x1800733b6`). The four
centroid roundings add128 as dwords, sign-extend, and shift right8; the moment
roundings add32768 as qwords before signed right16
(`0x1800733cb..0x18007343d`).

enrolled record; the supported 150-record limit therefore bounds the combined
pair count. Multiple enrolled records may map to the same probe record, so this
bound does not require distinct probe indexes or image-sized coordinates.
