## Input And Output Contract

For each source index, the selector consumes a best target index plus best and
second descriptor distances. A negative target index is unset. Profile-9/type-12
selection requires the strict signed-dword cross-product
`best_distance * 40 < second_distance * 38`. Reached descriptor distances are
`0..192`, so this multiplication does not overflow for production inputs.

Accepted pairs are emitted compactly as `(source_index,target_index)` in source
scan order, subject to replacement and swap-removal below. Every unused output
slot is filled with `-1,-1`. Capacity is caller-selected: profile-9/type-12
primary, alternate, and sibling builders use 42; relation and fallback builders
use 31. Selection is at `0x180075ae8..0x180075cec`, full-capacity replacement is
around `0x180075c30`, and publication/fill is at

## Spatial Collision Arithmetic

Target coordinates are loaded unsigned from record offsets `+2/+4`. Each
coordinate difference, square, and the sum of squares uses dword arithmetic;
the sum wraps modulo 2^32 and is interpreted as signed for the comparison at
`0x180075b90..0x180075bc3`. A pair is spatially distinct when that signed sum is
at least `0x10000`; equality is distinct. Consequently, accepted synthetic
16-bit coordinates such as `0xffff` and zero can produce a negative wrapped sum
and collide. Canonical profile-9 records use in-image Q8 coordinates, for which
the squared sum cannot overflow a signed dword.

Current production source applies this signed-dword rule to all five selector
implementations in `correspondence.c`. It is independent of the guided
affine-width difference documented in `FUN_180075dc0.md`.

## Replacement, Pruning, And Capacity

The selector scans already selected pairs in their current array order:

- A first collision with an equal or better selected distance rejects the
  incoming candidate and terminates its scan.
- A first collision with a worse selected distance is replaced in place.
- After replacement, a later equal-or-better collision neither undoes the
  replacement nor terminates the scan.
- After replacement, each later worse collision visited by the forward scan is
  removed by moving the final selected entry into its slot and decrementing the
  selected count. The scan then advances, so the moved entry is not rechecked.

This last detail can leave a worse colliding tail entry selected. It also means
post-replacement continuation can prune a later entry even after passing an
intervening equal-or-better collision. The state is production-reachable through
`FUN_180075220` and `FUN_1800749c0`, although the retained profile-9 operations
contain no natural trigger. That corpus absence is not an unreachability claim.
Current production source follows this continuation and pruning behavior in the
cross-class helper.

When capacity is full, the worst scan updates only for a strict increase, so the
first maximum owns a tie. The incoming candidate replaces that slot only when
its best distance is strictly lower. The selector itself has no capacity-status
path and its local storage covers 42 entries; a caller-supplied capacity above
42 is malformed and can overwrite the native stack rather than produce a
recoverable failure.
