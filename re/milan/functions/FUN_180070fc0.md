  clears low bits in the final eight columns and rewrites columns before them

- When the apply-mask flag is set, column `width-6` marks mask presence and

  post-render decision and apply-mask is the independent residual-mask flag

- The first call follows candidate publication, post-render status selection,

- The function changes only pixel low bits. It cannot explain multi-level
