## Normalization And Layout

For each radius, `total` is a wrapping signed-dword sum of every bin, including
the terminal transition class. For each published one-bit class, native computes

```text
total == 0 ? count << 12 : ((count << 12) + (total >> 1)) / total
```

All shifts and additions are dword operations. The nonzero division is signed
`IDIV`, truncating toward zero. Profile-9 counts keep every intermediate
positive and below signed-dword overflow, so this is ordinary nonnegative
Q12 half-up normalization.

The terminal transition bins are omitted from the output but remain in their
radius denominator. The 51 outputs are concatenated as radius-1 classes
`0..8` at indices `0..8`, radius-2 classes `0..16` at indices `9..25`, and
radius-3 classes `0..24` at indices `26..50`. A radius block can therefore sum
to less than 4096 when transition-class samples exist.
